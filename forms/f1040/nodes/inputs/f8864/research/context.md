# Form 8864, TY2025

See the
[current source and filing gap](../../../../../../docs/mef/ty2025/domains/credits/form8864/ty2025-form8864-gap.md),
the [December 2025 Form 8864](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8864.pdf), and
its [instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8864).

Only a qualified small agri-biodiesel producer credit survives for direct 2025
sales or use. Lines 7 and 8 apply $0.10 and $0.20 per gallon based on sale/use
date. The legacy $1.00 biodiesel, renewable-diesel and SAF rates are expired for
2025 direct production. The public source records a bounded registered Schedule
C producer and eligible dated sales, calculates Form 8864 lines, and keeps
export closed until native MeF and Form 3800 support exist.
