# General section map

General holds filer identity, whole-return assembly (Form 1040, Schedules 1
and 3), and informational/disclosure forms that carry no tax amount. This file
is the crosswalk for every General role tree; each other General folder has a
short AGENTS.md that points back here.

Mechanics that apply to the whole section:

- The MeF and PDF builders read the executor's pending dict, which is keyed by
  node type, so a node with `outputNodes = new OutputNodes([])` still reaches
  its serializer: the start node puts its validated input under its own node
  type. Serializers are registered in `ALL_MEF_FORMS`
  ([mef/forms/index.ts](../../../2025/mef/forms/index.ts)) and
  [pdf/forms/index.ts](../../../2025/pdf/forms/index.ts).
- An informational form with no serializer must not vanish from an export.
  [attachment_coverage.ts](../../../2025/return-processing/attachment_coverage.ts)
  lists the ones that block MeF/PDF export when present (f4547, f8997, f8958,
  f8275, f8833, f8938, f1310, f2120, f8332, f8082, f8867, `amendment_request`).
  The MeF and PDF builders also call `assertForm8858FilingSource`.
  [unregistered_attachment_export.test.ts](../../../e2e/workflows/unregistered_attachment_export.test.ts)
  checks this.
- Registry and CLI input surface do not always match (all exceptions are
  baselined in `scripts/testing/structure-baseline.json`). `f2120` and
  `f8275` are CLI inputs that no registry holds; the start node still writes
  their input to pending, so the export block applies. `f114`, `f8594`,
  `f14039` and `f911` are registered but are not CLI inputs, so
  `executeReturn` rejects them as unrecognized keys.
- Form 1040, Schedule 1 and Schedule 3 take values from Income, Adjustments,
  Credits and Payments. A focused line test lives in the section of the
  line's tax effect; informational foreign forms stay in General.

## Flow

- **`general` (filer identity, filing status, dependents)** sends values to 18
  nodes (`outputNodes` in [filing/general/index.ts](filing/general/index.ts)):
  `f1040`, `schedule1`, `agi_aggregator`, `standard_deduction`, `schedule_a`,
  `schedule1a`, `eitc`, `f8812`, `form8880`, `form8962`, `form8995`,
  `form8582`, `form461`, `form4137`, `form8919`, `form8959`, `form8960`,
  `form5329`. Filing status and dependent tests reach Credits, Deductions,
  Taxes and Income from here.
- **`preparer`** sends only `preparer_*` fields to `f1040`;
  `extractFilerIdentity` in [mef/filer.ts](../../../mef/filer.ts) reads them
  for the MeF header.
- **Schedule 1 and AGI.** Income and adjustment nodes route the same line
  field to both `schedule1` (printed lines) and `agi_aggregator`, which
  computes `line8_additional_income`, `line9_total_income`,
  `line10_adjustments` and `line11_agi` for `f1040`. It also sends AGI/MAGI to
  `standard_deduction`, `schedule_a`, `schedule1a`, `eitc`, `f8812`, `f2441`,
  `form8995`, `form8960`, `form8962`, `form8880`, `form_1116`,
  `schedule_j_calculation` and `form8582`. `schedule1` computes nothing that
  `f1040` reads.
- **Passive-loss two pass.** When `pal_pending_active_4797` is true,
  `agi_aggregator` emits only `form8582` (modified AGI) and `agi_final`.
  `form8582` returns the allowed losses to `agi_final`, which re-runs
  `agi_aggregator.compute` with the final figures and drops the `form8582`
  output.
- **Schedule 3.** Credits nodes (e.g. `form8396`, `form2441`, `form8880`,
  `form5695`, `form_1116`, `form8962`, `f3800`, `f8863`, `schedule_r`) and
  Part II sources (`ext`, `f2439`, and `w2` for `line11_excess_ss`) feed
  `schedule3`. It sends
  `line20_nonrefundable_credits`, `line31_additional_payments` and
  `credit_limit_schedule3_lines` to `f1040`, and writes its print totals
  (`line8_total`, `line15_total`, ...) back to its own pending entry.
- **`f1040` finalizes earlier nodes.** It applies the credit-limit worksheet
  and returns `finalizations` that replace the pending values of `schedule3`,
  `form8880`, `form8396`, `f8912`, `f8859`, `f8834`, `f3800` and `f2210f`
  (`core/runtime/executor.ts` merges these with replace semantics). A credit's
  printed amount can therefore differ from what its own node emitted.
- **Cross-section effects of General inputs.** `f5471` sends Schedule 1 lines
  8n (§951(a)) and 8o (§951A GILTI) to `schedule1` and `agi_aggregator`
  (Income effect). `f8958` sends `f8958_allocation` to Income `w2`.
  `schedule_b_part_iii` feeds Income `schedule_b`. `f8854` deemed gain is
  reported through Form 8949 rows that the MeF builder reconciles. `f8997`
  reconciles against Form 8949 transactions
  ([reconciliation.ts](investments/f8997/reconciliation.ts)). Credits
  `form5695` emits `joint_occupancy_statement`. Every other input in this
  section only validates its input (`outputs: []`).
