# Deductions — section map

Deductions after AGI: Schedule A and its sources (Form 1098, Form 8283,
casualty Form 4684, investment interest Form 4952), the standard deduction
worksheet that produces Form 1040 lines 12–15, Schedule 1-A (line 13b), QBI
(Forms 8995/8995-A, line 13) and the business deduction forms 4562, 8829 and
8990. This file is the map for every deductions folder in every role tree;
each of those folders links back here.

`forms/f1040/2025/registry.ts`; "CLI" marks an `inputNodes` entry in

## Flow

Upstream fields arrive by `outputNodes` routing; this section's nodes then route:

- **Schedule A** (`schedule_a`) collects `line_5a_sales_tax`
  (`sales_tax_deduction`), `line_8a_mortgage_interest_1098` and
  `home_mortgage_nonqualifying_use_review` (`f1098`), `line_8c_points_no_1098`
  (`mortgage_refinance_points`), `noncash_contribution_items` (`f8283`),
  `line_15_casualty_theft_loss` (`form4684`), `line_9_investment_interest`
  (`form4952`), plus deposits from other sections (`w2`
  `line_5a_state_income_tax`, `f1099r` `retirement_state_local_withholding`,
  `ltc_premium` `line_1_medical`, `f2106`/`k1_s_corp`/`form4972`
  `line_16_other_deductions`, `form8396` credit reduction, `general`
  `filing_status`, `agi_aggregator` `agi`). It routes `itemized_deductions`,
  `itemized_taxes`, `itemized_investment_interest` and
  `niit_allocable_state_local_tax` to `standard_deduction`,
  `itemized_deductions` to `form8995`, and `line3_charitable_contribution_adjustment`
  to `form6251`; its own lines 11–13 come back as `finalizations`, and
  charitable carryovers as `carryforwards`.
- **Standard deduction worksheet** (`standard_deduction`) takes `agi`, filing
  status and age/blind flags, the Schedule A total, `qbi_deduction` (from
  `form8995`/`form8995a`) and `additional_deductions` (from `schedule1a`); its
  `nol_deduction` field has no producer (the NOL enters AGI as Schedule 1
  line 8a). It picks standard vs itemized and routes
  `line12a_standard_deduction` or `line12e_itemized_deductions` and
  `line15_taxable_income` to `f1040`, `taxable_income` and
  `form6251_line1b`/`form6251_line2a` to `income_tax_calculation`, and feeds
  `schedule_j_calculation`, `form_1116` and (itemizing only) `form8960`
  lines 9a/9b.
- **Schedule 1-A** (`schedule1a`, MAGI from `agi_aggregator`, tips/overtime from
  income nodes) routes `line13b_additional_deductions` and
  `schedule1a_line37_senior_deduction` to `f1040`, the senior and vehicle-interest
  parts to `standard_deduction` (excluded from Form 1116 line 3b and added back
  for Form 6251 line 1b), and `additional_deductions` plus
  `qualified_tip_qbi_source` to `form8995` (taxable income before QBI).
- **QBI**: `form8995` collects deposits from `schedule_c`, `schedule_f`,
  `f4835`, `schedule_e`, `k1_partnership`, `k1_s_corp`, `w2`, `f1099div`,
  `f1099int`, `f8814`, `schedule_d`/`schedule_d_final`, `schedule_se`,
  `form7206`, `sep_retirement`, `schedule_a`, `schedule1a`, `general` and
  `agi_aggregator`/`agi_final`; `qbi_patron` adds cooperative patron reviews. Above the taxable-income
  threshold (or with patron reviews) `form8995` routes to `form8995a`
  instead of filing itself; `qbi_aggregation` and `f1099div` also route to
  `form8995a`. Whichever files sends `line13_qbi_deduction` to `f1040` and
  `qbi_deduction` to `standard_deduction`; `form8995a` also deposits the
  pending slice of the Schedule A–D it used.
- **Form 4952** sends `line_9_investment_interest` to `schedule_a` (or, for a
  royalty debt trace, a Schedule E property row) and its line 4g election to
  `income_tax_calculation`; excess interest leaves as
  `investment_interest_excess_4952` / `amt_investment_interest_excess_4952`
  carryforwards.
- **Business forms**: `form_8829` sends `form8829_line30` to `schedule_c`;
  `form4562` and `form8990` route nowhere (`outputNodes` empty). They only
  write their own pending slice, which the MeF/PDF projections reconcile against
  `schedule_c`, `schedule1` and `f1040`. Active `form8990` sources run only
  through `runBoundedForm8990TwoPass` from `executeReturn` in
  `forms/f1040/2025/index.ts`.

## Cross-section effects

- **Taxes** (Form 6251 AMT): `f59e` → `line2o_circulation_costs` /
  `other_adjustments`; `f1098` → `line3_houseboat_interest_addback`;
  `schedule_a` → line 3 charitable adjustment; `standard_deduction` →
  `form6251_line1b`/`line2a`; `form4952` AMT refigure and line 4g election →
  `income_tax_calculation`; `standard_deduction` → `form8960` (NIIT).
- **Income**: `f1098` refunds of prior-year interest →
  `line8z_f1098_interest_recovery` (Schedule 1 line 8z and `agi_aggregator`);
  `form4684` business losses → `form4797` `ordinary_gain_form4684` (§1231) or
  `schedule_d` `line_11_form2439`; `form_8829` → `schedule_c`; `form4952` →
  `schedule_e`; `qbi_patron` → `schedule_c`/`schedule_f`/`w2`. Business
  sources that feed QBI and depreciation live under Income
  (`../income/business/`, `../income/rental-passthrough/`).
- **Credits**: `standard_deduction` → `form_1116` (worldwide taxable income).
- Form 4562 and Forms 8995/8995-A are deduction families even where the
  source document (Schedule C, K-1) is in Income.

## Known gaps in the graph

- `f8903` is registered but neither a CLI input nor routed to; it is baselined
  as unreachable in `scripts/testing/structure-baseline.json`.
- `form8995a_schedule_a`/`_c`/`_d` are registered under camelCase keys
  (`form8995aScheduleA`, …) and `form8995a_schedule_b` is not registered
  (both baselined). The executor resolves `registry[nodeType]`, so these
  nodes never run; `form8995a`'s outputs still fill their pending slices for
  MeF/PDF.
- K-1 and Schedule E nodes deposit `section_179_deduction` into `form4562`,
  which rejects that aggregate with "Form 4562 aggregate-only inputs cannot
  establish native asset rows…".
