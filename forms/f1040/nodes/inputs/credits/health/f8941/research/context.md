# TY2025 Form 8941 owned employer source

The source models one materially participating Schedule C proprietor, one employment EIN, one full-year employee-only SHOP plan and Albany County, NY. It retains identified employee payroll hours/wages and twelve employer-owned monthly invoices/payments per employee, with unique references, matching employee SSNs, EIN and plan. Employer contributions are uniform and at least 50%. Excluded owner/family/seasonal workers, other businesses/common control and state subsidies are outside this route.

[2025 IRS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i8941--2025.pdf) provide Worksheets 1–7, a $9,358 employee-only Albany average premium, fewer than25 FTEs and average wages below$67,000. FTEs floor at2080 hours; wages floor to$1,000. Both phaseout reductions subtract from the original credit; Worksheet 6 uses$33,300. The ordinary employer rate is50%. Form 8941 line 16 reaches Form 3800 Part III4h, then Schedule 3 line6a and Form 1040 line20 subject to the section 38 limit.

[26 USC 280C(h)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapB-partIX-sec280C.htm) and [26 CFR 1.45R-5(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.45R-5) disallow premiums by the credit determined under 45R(a), before the tax-use limit. The public graph therefore retains gross Schedule C benefits, derives the full credit reduction, and calculates SE/QBI/1040 afterward. A separately finalized Form 3800 allocation records current tax use. Full, partial and zero current tax use all retain the same premium reduction.

The held fixture is synthetic first-year 2025 source evidence. It does not assert authentic issuer records or prior IRS acceptance. Other plans/rating areas, excluded employees, family coverage, tax-exempt employers, mixed business-credit allocations and prior-period authentication remain separate proof requirements.
