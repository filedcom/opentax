# Credits section map

Credit claims and their limitations: Form 1040 line 19 (Schedule 8812), lines
27–29 (EIC, ACTC, refundable AOTC), Schedule 3 Part I (nonrefundable, lines
1–6m) and Part II lines 9 and 12 (premium tax credit, fuel tax credit). Nine
role trees hold this section; this file is the crosswalk between them.

## Flow

- **Refundable → Form 1040.** `f8812` → `f1040` `line19_child_tax_credit` and
  `line28_actc`; `eitc` → `line27_eitc`; `f8863` → `line29_refundable_aoc` and
  Schedule 3 `line3_education_credit`. `form8962` → Schedule 3 `line9` (net
  PTC); `f4136` → Schedule 3 `line12`.
- **Direct Schedule 3 Part I.** `form_1116` → `line1`; `f2441`/`form2441` →
  `line2` (and `line1e_taxable_dep_care` to `f1040`); `form5695` → `line5a`/`5b`;
  `f8801` → `line6b`; `schedule_r` → `line6d`; `f8936` personal → `line6f`/`6m`;
  `f8911` personal → `line6j`.
- **Tax-limited credits finalize in the `f1040` sink**
  (`../../outputs/general/return-assembly/f1040/index.ts`), not in their own
  node. The node sends a source (`form8880_source` via Schedule 3,
  `form8834_source_credit`, `form8396_source`, `form8859_source_carryforward`,
  `form3800_source_credits`, `form8912_source_lines`) and marks Schedule 3
  `*_source_credit_pending`. The sink computes, in order: 8880 → 8834 → 8936
  clean vehicles → 8396 → 8859 → 3800 Part II → 8912, each against line 16 tax
  and the Form 6251 lines reported back as `credit_limit_form6251_line9/11`.
  It uses `f3800/calculation.ts`, `f8912/calculation.ts`,
  `schedule_r/calculation.ts`, `form8396/calculation.ts` and
  `form8880/calculation.ts`.
- **Business credits fan in to `f3800`**: see [business/AGENTS.md](business/AGENTS.md).
  Passive ones go through `form8582cr` first; `f8826` goes through
  `disabled_access_limit`.
- **Gates.** `f8862` sets `form8862_filed` on `eitc`, `f8812` and `f8863`.
  `f1095a`/`qsehra` feed `form8962`.

### Cross-section effects

- Taxes: `form8962` excess APTC → `schedule2` `line1a_excess_advance_premium`
  (the whole family stays in Credits); `f8936` dealer-transfer repayment →
  `schedule2` `line1b`/`line1c`; `f8874_recapture` → `schedule2`
  `line17a_new_markets_credit_recapture`. `f8801`, `f8834`, `f8864`, `f8911`,
  `f8912`, `f3800`, `form_1116`, `form8962` all write to `form6251` (AMT
  filing flags, the AMT foreign tax credit, Schedule 2 tax for AMT).
- Income: `f5884`, `f8994` (→ `schedule_c`) and `f8941` (→ `schedule_c`,
  `schedule_f`) reduce wage or premium deductions; `f5884` also reduces
  `schedule_f`. `f8912` reports bond interest on `schedule_b`; `qsehra` adds
  `line1a_wages`; `form2441` sends taxable benefits to `agi_aggregator`.
- Deductions: `form8396` reduces `schedule_a` mortgage interest
  (`form8396_interest_credit_reduction`).
- Inbound: `form_1116` reads `f1099int`, `f1099div`, `fec`, the three K-1
  nodes, `income_tax_calculation` and `standard_deduction`; `form8962` reads
  `general`, `w2`, `f8814`, `form7206` and the AGI aggregators.
