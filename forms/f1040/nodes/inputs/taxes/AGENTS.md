# Taxes section map

Tax on taxable income, additional taxes (Schedule 2), recapture, interest
charges and underpayment penalties. This file is the crosswalk for the whole
section; each role folder has a short AGENTS.md that links back here.

Node types are registry keys in [`2025/registry.ts`](../../../2025/registry.ts);
"CLI" means the node has an `inputNodes` entry in
[`2025/inputs.ts`](../../../2025/inputs.ts). Paths are relative to this folder.

## Flow

Every arrow below is an `outputNodes` route, read from the node files.

- **Schedule 2** (`schedule2`) collects every additional tax. Part I (line 2
  AMT plus lines 1a–1f) → `f1040.line17_additional_taxes` and
  `credit_limit_schedule2_line1z` (Part I minus AMT); Part II (line 21) →
  `f1040.line23_other_taxes`. It also sends both part totals and the
  chapter 1 split to `form8978_reporting_year`. Line 20 (Form 965-A) is in the
  schema but excluded from line 21, as the form requires.
- Into Schedule 2 from this section: `form6251` line 2; `f4255` lines 1d/1e/1f
  and 19; `schedule_se` line 4; `form4137` line 5; `form8919` line 6;
  `form5329` line 8 (+ chapter 1 portion); `schedule_h` line 9; `form8959`
  line 11; `form8960` line 12; `f453a_interest` line 15; `f8611` line 16
  (Form 8611 line 14); `f8828` line 17b; `f965` line 20. `f5405` routes line 10
  only before TY2025 and Schedule 2 rejects line 10 for 2025.
- **Line 16 tax**: `income_tax_calculation` (Tax Table under $100,000,
  Tax Computation Worksheet above, QDCGT/Schedule D worksheet inside
  `preferential_tax.ts`) → `f1040.line16_income_tax`. Add-ons arrive as fields:
  `form4972_tax` (`form4972`), `form8978_tax` (positive Form 8978 line 14 from
  `f8978`), `form8615_source` (`f8615`), `schedule_j_calculated_tax`
  (`schedule_j_calculation`, after `schedule_j` requests the election),
  `rate_28_gain`/`unrecaptured_1250_gain` (via `rate_28_gain_worksheet` →
  `qdcgtw`). It then feeds `form6251` (regular tax), `form8615` (finalized
  Form 8615 lines, which `form8615` only validates and keeps for export),
  `form8978_reporting_year`, `f8812` and `form_1116`.
- **AMT**: `form6251` → Schedule 2 line 2 and `f1040`
  `credit_limit_form6251_line9`/`line11`. It is routed from 26 nodes, nearly all
  outside this section (1099-INT/DIV/OID, 3921, 8949, K-1s, Schedule A/C/E,
  8801, 3800, 8911, …).
- **Employment taxes**: `form4137` → `f1040`/`agi_aggregator` line 1c,
  `schedule1a`, Schedule 2 line 5, `form8959` (Medicare tips) and `form8919`
  (`form4137_sources`, shared SS wage base). `form8919` → line 1g, Schedule 2
  line 6, `schedule_se` (`wages_8919`), `form8959`. `ct2` → `form8959`.
  `form8959` → Schedule 2 line 11 and `f1040.line25c_additional_medicare_withheld`.
- **Schedule SE** → Schedule 2 line 4, `schedule1` line 15 and
  `agi_aggregator` (deduction), `eitc`, `form8959` (`se_income`), `form8995`,
  `form7206`, `schedule1a`.
- **Form 8978**: `f8978` → `income_tax_calculation` (positive), `form6251` and
  `form8978_reporting_year` (negative line 14). The worksheet →
  `schedule3.line6l_form8978_credit` and
  `f1040.form8978_schedule2_line17z_reduction`.
- **Penalties**: `f2210` sends either a penalty (`line38_underpayment_penalty`)
  or `f2210_active` + payment facts that `f1040` turns into line 38 with its
  final total tax; `f2210f` sends `f2210f_box_b_source`, whose line 16 takes
  precedence in `f1040`.
- **No route yet**: `f8697`, `f8866` always throw; `f4970` throws on any
  positive amount; `f8965` returns nothing (mandate penalty is $0 since 2019).
  `return-processing/attachment_coverage.ts` also blocks export for `f8697`,
  `f8866`, `f8828` and Form 2210 Part II reasons (waiver, annualized, box D,
  box E), and blocks only the PDF for `f965`, `f4255`, and `f8611` items
  without an `issuer_source`.

## Cross-section effects

- Form 4972 is canonically a Taxes form, but its source is `f1099r`
  (`inputs/income/retirement/`); it also routes `line5b_form4972_ordinary` to
  `f1040`/`agi_aggregator` and the estate-tax share to `scheduleA`
  `line_16_other_deductions`. Forms 4255/8611/8828 recapture are Taxes; the
  original credits live under Credits.
- Into Schedule 2 from other sections: Form 8962 line 1a (Credits/health),
  `f8936` lines 1b/1c, `f8874` recapture line 17a (Credits), Forms 8889/8853
  lines 17c–17f (Adjustments/health), `w2` lines 13/17h/17k, `f1099m` 17h,
  `f1099nec` 17k, `f8621` 17p/17q/17z (Income). The MeF Schedule 2 serializer
  imports their pure helpers from those node folders.
- Into `income_tax_calculation` from other sections: Schedule D, `f1099div`,
  K-1 partnership, `f8814`, `f8621`, Form 4952, Form 2555, standard deduction.
- Into `schedule_se`: Schedule C, Schedule F, Form 2555, `k1_partnership`,
  `w2`. Into `form5329`: `f1099r`, `f4852`, `general`, Forms 8889/8853. Into
  `form8960`: Schedules B/D/E, `k1_partnership`, `f1099div`, `f1099m`,
  `f8814`, `f8621`, `general`, the AGI nodes and the standard deduction.
- Nodes here import year code: `f2210`, `f2210f`, `form6251` and the `f1040`
  output import calculations from `2025/domains/taxes/underpayment/` and
  `2025/domains/taxes/amt/form6251/form6251_prior_iso_sale.ts`.
