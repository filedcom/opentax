# OpenTax v2.0.8-checkpoint.1

Development checkpoint of the integrated TY2025 Form 1040 work through October 7, 2026.

- Includes source-backed investment-interest and AMT private-activity-bond reconciliation, direct-owner QBI annual disclosures, dependent MAGI from multiple sources, and larger or zero-income-spouse joint Form 8962 families.
- Retains the earlier retirement, child-income, business, basis, household payroll and supporting native/PDF work documented in the completed ledger and source proofs.
- Native binaries for five platforms must pass the compiled W-2/MeF/filled-PDF smoke before publication; SHA256SUMS accompanies the assets.
- Positive-spouse Form 8962, issuer RPE aggregation and joint-owner royalties remain on private checkpoint branches for subsequent verification and integration.

## Qualification

This is a prerelease development checkpoint. The 52 existing broad TODOs remain open; full regression at the latest production state, source authentication, IRS business rules, ATS enrollment/scenario conflicts and accepted IRS acknowledgments are incomplete. Focused source-route tests and local XSD success do not establish filing readiness or IRS acceptance.
