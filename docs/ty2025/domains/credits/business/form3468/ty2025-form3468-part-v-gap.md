# TY2025 Form 3468 Part V trust statement route

The [2025 beneficiary Schedule K-1 (Form 1041) instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1041sk1)
place the orphan-drug credit at **box 13 code M** and the clean electricity
investment-credit information at **box 14 code M**. The fiduciary supplies a
property-level statement with the beneficiary's amounts needed for Form 3468
Part V. The box 14 code is information; its amount is not treated as a computed
credit. The [2025 Form 3468 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i3468)
require a separate Form 3468 for each facility/property and Part V line 11 is
reported at [Form 3800 Part III line 1v](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i3800).

The bounded positive route accepts a trust-owned solar facility placed in
service in 2025 with reviewed zero-emissions generation, less than 1 MW AC
output, 30% base rate, beneficiary allocated qualified basis, and an
independently reviewed fiduciary property statement. The K-1 box 14 code M
marker, statement, separate review packet, issuer EIN, beneficiary SSN,
property reference, and source PDF SHA-256 metadata must agree. Form 3468
calculates Part V lines 1a–2 and 5–11; Form 3800 applies the nonpassive line
1v credit through its tax limitation to Schedule 3 and Form 1040. One native
IRS3468 and one printable Form 3468 are retained per property. Direct legacy
Form 3468 basis claims remain blocked at export.

The reviewed packet contains a digest and source reference, but this route has
no executor-owned K-1 issuer-copy byte verifier. It must not be described as
authenticating the underlying PDF bytes. Broader Form 3468 sections, facility
types, 6% or wage-based rate, low-income/domestic/energy-community bonuses,
financing reduction, interconnection property, transfers, elective payment,
cooperatives, passive activity, multi-year credits and recapture remain open.
Native/XSD/PDF rendering and filing business rules await the combined
implementation-first validation pass.
