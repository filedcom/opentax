# TY2025 Form 4972: same-beneficiary multiple issued partial shares

Page 3, “Multiple recipients of a lump-sum distribution,” uses box 9a to gross the cash distribution and allocate the residual Part III tax, while box 8 has its own percentage for the annuity value. The NUA Worksheet uses combined box 3 divided by combined box 2a to allocate combined box 6 between capital and ordinary amounts.

Each positive gross box 1 equals box 2a plus box 6. Source amounts and recipient/full-plan allocations are exact whole dollars. The public election names every issued copy, the participant and plan; it supplies no replacement distribution amounts or percentages.
