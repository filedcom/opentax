# TY2025 MeF ATS preparation

This is an engineering checklist, not an IRS approval or transmission package.
No test return has been sent to ATS.

## Parallel acceptance gates

These can be prepared while the Form 1040 calculation gaps are being built, but
none is an ATS acceptance. Do not transmit until all three lanes are complete.

| Lane               | Current evidence                                                                                                                                                                    | Remaining gate                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| IRS administration | No issued ATS certificate, Automated Enrollment registration, ASID, authorized ETIN, or endpoint package is confirmed in this repo. No secret values were inspected for this audit. | Obtain the issued ATS public certificate and confirm its private key match, register the certificate, record the ASID and authorized ETIN, and receive the ATS endpoint/WSDL/trust package.                                                        |
| A2A client         | The repo builds an offline submission ZIP and `SendSubmissionsRequest` body with an outer attachment ZIP.                                                                           | Build and validate the complete signed SOAP/MeF header, strong-authentication and MTOM transport, login/session handling, GetAcks retrieval, and durable acknowledgment storage. The local return XSD bundle does not include the A2A service XSD. |
| Test return        | TY2025 Form 1040 ATS scenario source facts are partial, with unresolved source contradictions and no complete business-rule-verified scenario.                                      | Assemble a complete supported scenario, pass the calculation/XML/PDF batch, submit it to ATS, and archive its accepted acknowledgment.                                                                                                             |

[IRS Publication 5830](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5830.pdf) describes the
MeF header, strong authentication, submission, and acknowledgment services.
Packaging bytes alone does not satisfy any of these acceptance gates.

### Schedule 8812 filing facts

The Schedule 8812 calculator and MeF builder require the complete Credit Limit
Worksheet A inputs for Schedule 3 lines 1, 2, 3, 4, 5b, 6d, 6f, 6l, and 6m. They
also require an explicit answer on whether Worksheet B applies and, if so, its
line 15 total. The partial engine-fed credit shortcut has been removed. Supply
this worksheet once on the public `f8812` item or in internal pending data; the
tax graph does not yet assemble every required line automatically.

For Part II-A, provide verified Schedule 8812 line 18a earned income directly or
supply the complete `earned_income_worksheet`. The worksheet covers signed farm
and nonfarm results, the optional farm method, Medicaid waiver exclusions, and
the Schedule 1 line 15 deduction. W-2 wages plus Schedule C profit alone are not
enough. When EIC applies, select the line 18a method required by the IRS Earned
Income Chart. Conflicting direct and worksheet amounts are rejected. For Part
II-B, provide the separate W-2 withholding, Schedule 1, Schedule 2, Form 1040
EIC, and Schedule 3 adoption-credit amounts. See the
[2025 form](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1040s8.pdf) and
[instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i1040s8.pdf).

1040-SS Scenario 6 supplies three qualifying children, Puerto Rico wages and
withholding, a 3,000 prior-year payment application, and selected Schedule C
expenses and inventory entries. The printed Schedule C gross receipts and
computed lines are blank, so its net profit and self-employment tax cannot be
asserted from this PDF alone. The repo has no standalone 1040-SS XML exporter.

1040-NR Scenario 12 prints a 375,000 partnership transfer with 25,000 outside

only 5,000 carried to the return. The printed 1040-NR balances 90,297 total tax
against 90,297 estimated payments. These are source facts, not a generated NR
return or a validation of its tax calculation.

1040-NR Scenario 1's cover sheet says the IRA distribution was 4,500 against a

figures are captured separately. Its two W-2s total 33,855 wages and 4,788

computes.

1040-NR Scenario 2 captures its 25,988 W-2 wages and 2,916 withholding, the

1040-NR Scenario 3 prints 72,102 W-2 wages, 21,750 federal withholding, and

1040-NR Scenario 4 captures the 2024 GMC Sierra, its 3,500 tentative credit and

Scenario 1's source W-2 amounts are 22,970 and 19,500 in wages, with 1,073 and
1,640 in federal withholding. Schedule H has 3,100 in cash wages, employer EIN
000000029, and an affirmative line A answer. Form 5695 lists three exterior
doors totaling 2,740 and separately prints another 2,740 on line 19e ("all
other" doors), which should not be silently reconciled; it also lists 600 in
windows and 2,500 in central air conditioning. These values were used in a local
diagnostic run. The Schedule H source slice now passes the v5.4 XSD, but Form
5695's sourced extra-item facts remain incomplete.

The 2025 return builder now offers `buildMefBundle` for XML plus validated PDF
bytes. It counts each `BinaryAttachment` in the return header and return data,
checks the physical PDF, and keeps the exact PDF filename alongside its XML
metadata. This is a logical bundle, not a MeF transmission ZIP. The XML-only
builder rejects Form 5695 claims that need a PDF. For doors beyond the three
printed item rows, windows beyond the four printed rows, and Section B items
beyond their printed QMID rows, the bundle generates
`AdditionalQMIDStatement.pdf` with the IRS-recommended description
`Additional QMID Statement` and lists the QMID and cost of each overflow item.
The name comes from the
[IRS TY2025 recommended PDF names workbook](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-efile/tax-year-2025-recommended-pdf-names-attached-mef-1040-series-extensions-submissions.xlsx).
`buildMefSubmissionArchive` now takes a real filer identity, explicit Software
ID and EFIN, a 20-character Submission ID, its UTC processing date, and the
pending return. It builds the logical bundle and a compressed
`<SubmissionId>.zip` containing `manifest/manifest.xml`, `xml/submission.xml`,
and, when needed, `attachment/*.pdf`. Both XML files begin with the IRS-required
UTF-8 declaration. The manifest passes the local `efileAttachments.xsd`. The
Submission ID must start with the EFIN, processing year, and Julian day.
`buildMefTransmissionPackage` takes 1 to 100 submission ZIPs with explicit
electronic postmarks and returns the `SendSubmissionsRequest` SOAP body element
plus an uncompressed outer ZIP for the A2A attachment. This request element
follows Publication 5830's published shape, but has not been checked against the
A2A service XSD, which is not in the local TY2025 return-schema bundle. Both
builders return bytes or XML only; they do not write files, sign, transmit, or
deploy. See [Publication 4164](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p4164.pdf) and
[Publication 5830](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5830.pdf) for the archive
and A2A request requirements. The full SOAP envelope, certificate
authentication, IRS registration values, business-rule validation, and ATS
acknowledgments remain open. Do not treat packaged bytes alone as
transmission-ready.

Schedule H now computes Part I's Additional Medicare Tax from supplied
per-employee wages above the $200,000 withholding threshold, and Part II Section
B from state wage, rate-period, and contribution facts. For TY2025 it also
applies Worksheet 1's 90% late-payment credit and Worksheet 2's California
(1.2%) and U.S. Virgin Islands (4.5%) credit-reduction rates, then routes line
26 to Schedule 2 line 9. The generated Section B XML and Section A's explicit 0%
state experience-rate code pass the local v5.4 XSD. This is synthetic coverage,
not a complete Scenario 1 fixture or an ATS business-rule acknowledgment.

Scenario 4's PDF supplies Form 3800 Part III entries of 13,200 from Form 8835
and 130 from Form 8936, plus a 2024 BMW i4 Gran Coupe on Schedule A (Form 8936).
The PDF prints `APPLD FOR` in Form 3800's transferor-EIN column; the fixture
records that literal entry rather than treating it as a valid EIN. These source
entries are not independently computed 1040 credits. The binary attachment is
described as `Transfer Election Statement`, but its content is not in the
scenario PDF.

Scenario 5's PDF supplies two dependent children, 1,820 total child-care
spending across two providers, 980 adjusted qualified education expenses, and
1,475 moving expenses. It checks both the Schedule 1 line 14 Armed Forces box
and the 1040 line 28 opt-out of the additional child tax credit. These are
source entries, not a determination that the moving expense deduction or any
credit is allowable. Form 8862 has some unanswered boxes, so the fixture only
captures the answered questions.

Scenario 12 prints a full set of 1040 totals, unlike most scenario PDFs. Its
15,000 single standard deduction is internally consistent with its 107,445
taxable income and 7,628 amount owed, but differs from the current 2025
standard-deduction configuration of 15,750. The test preserves both values so
this source/version discrepancy cannot silently change the implementation. The
PDF also rounds Schedule SE's Social Security and Medicare tax lines to 2,786
and 652 before summing to 3,438. The current node sums unrounded values and
rounds to 3,437. A source-matching test is pending until rounding behavior is
reviewed across returns; it must not be reported as passing. The included Form
7217 is labeled revision December 2024 even though its distribution date is
March 2025. Its Part I line 10 shows 6,000 while Part II total column (e) shows
4,000, contrary to the line 10 instruction. Both printed values are retained.
Its W-2 checks Box 13 retirement-plan coverage but reports no IRA contribution.
That coverage fact now passes through the IRA worksheet without attempting a
deduction or producing an executor error. A positive IRA contribution still
requires filing status, MAGI, and employer-plan coverage.

Scenario 13 prints a 1,000 personal-use electric charger cost, a 300 tentative
credit, and a 162 allowed credit after its regular-tax limit. The latter flows
through Schedule 3 line 6j to 1040 line 20. Its 30,000 MFJ standard deduction
differs from the current 2025 configuration of 31,500, so its printed 1,620
taxable income and 162 tax must not be used to override current tax rules. The
Form 8911, its Schedule A, and Schedule 3 line 6j now have an XSD-validated
source slice. The current return calculation does not reproduce the PDF's
outdated standard deduction. The full scenario remains unvalidated against IRS
business rules.

The 1040 exporter now declares TY2025v5.4. The Form 6251 line-1 element was
updated to the v5.4 line 1b tag, and the local 1040 XSD suites pass against
Return1040.xsd v5.4. That proves structure for the covered XML slices, not ATS
business-rule acceptance or full support for every form. The return header still
uses placeholder Software ID and EFIN values until the IRS package and
registration details are supplied. Keep these as explicit pre-transmission
checks, not values to guess.

The Scenario 8 PDF checks 1040 line 4c QCD and line 5c rollover even though its
1099-Rs show codes Q and G with no IRA/SEP/SIMPLE indicator; the source-fact
capture retains those marks while the calculation fixture does not invent a QCD
or a separate rollover amount. The MFS lived-apart answer now reaches the Social
Security worksheet and line 6d XML in v5.4. Written cases cover both lived-apart
and lived-with-spouse taxability, but have not run in the deferred full batch;
the printed contradictions and IRS business-rule acceptance remain unresolved.

The v5.4 `JointOccupancyStatement` document now has a typed XML builder and XSD
test for Part I fuel-cell and Part II home-improvement allocations. Its
percentages and credits are calculated from the supplied payment facts. The Part
I fuel-cell path now routes the all-occupant payment through the input and
calculation nodes, allocates the filer cost, and links Form 5695 line 7c to the
statement's actual document ID. The return builder rejects dangling document
references and unreferenced joint-occupancy statements. Part II joint-occupancy
claims now route per-property all-occupant payments from itemized Sections A and
B and qualified audits, apply property and annual allocations, and link line 32a
to the same statement document. Sections A and B overflow have a separate PDF
statement in a MeF bundle. The multi-main-home rule remains unsupported. These
claims must not be represented as ATS-ready.

## Local XSD validation

1. Unpack the TY2025v5.4 schema from IMF_05-28-2026_Release-2.zip under

   IndividualIncomeTax/Ind1040/Return1040.xsd and

2. Run: deno test --allow-read --allow-write --allow-run=xmllint
   forms/f1040/e2e/xsd_validation.test.ts
   forms/f1040/2025/mef/xsd-validation.test.ts
   forms/f1040/2025/mef/submission-archive.xsd.test.ts

   forms/f1040/2025/mef/forms/schedule_8812.xsd.test.ts
3. Check that every selected test passes with 0 ignored. The schema tests skip
   when the local IRS schema bundle is missing.
4. For a separately prepared 1040-NR XML, run: xmllint --noout --schema
   .state/research/docs/IMF_Series_2025v5.4/1040x_Schema_2025v5.4/2025v5.4/IndividualIncomeTax/Ind1040NR/Return1040NR.xsd
   /absolute/path/to/return1040nr.xml

The current 1040 exporter declares returnVersion 2025v5.4. XSD success does not
prove business-rule acceptance, tax math, signature, header, or ATS transport
readiness. Each required scenario still needs complete inputs, computed
expectations, XML, local validation against the applicable schema, and an IRS
ATS acknowledgment after enrollment and credentials are available.
