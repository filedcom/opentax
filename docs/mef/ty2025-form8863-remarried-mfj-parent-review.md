# TY2025 remarried custodial MFJ parent and education source ownership

## Existing gap and official scope

The sibling checkpoint proved an unremarried custodial single parent and
never-married cohabiting single parents. Its reviewed parent source required
one primary wage owner and single status; paired education export also assumed
that the child's actual education claimant was the primary filer.

[2025 Form 8615 instructions, pages 1–2](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i8615--2025.pdf)
use the custodial parent's joint return with a new spouse after remarriage.
The stepparent replaces the noncustodial parent for this selection. Lines A/B
identify the person listed first on the joint return, including a stepparent;
line C uses joint status. Other eligible children sharing that tax parent enter
line 7 through their actual line 5 amounts. The parent's before-credit income
tax supplies line 10, and family tax does not recompute parent credits.

[2025 Form 8863 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i8863--2025.pdf)
permit education credits for an eligible claimed dependent and retain the
joint-return MAGI phaseout. The new source proof separates the custody/payment
owner from the first-listed joint tax identity. Neither child claims a duplicate
credit. The [2025 Form 1040 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i1040gi--2025.pdf)
supply the joint standard deduction and ordinary Tax Table/Tax Computation
Worksheet used by these actual wage returns.

## Calculation and source/export joins

The selected MFJ tax is recomputed from actual distinct parent/spouse-owned
issued wage sources, joint filing identities, combined AGI, ordinary joint
deduction and ordinary tax. Every wage owner must be a member of that actual
joint return. Primary and spouse names/TINs/DOBs bind the retained public
return and filer identity. The remarriage review separately binds custodial,
stepparent and excluded noncustodial identities and actual reviewed custody.
The first-listed joint identity is derived from the actual source return.

The existing education input contract still requires MAGI and credit-limit
worksheet values. This fixture obtains them from an actual public joint
own-income execution, then obtains the education credit from the public
education calculation before completing Schedule8812 priority inputs. No tax,
MAGI, phaseout ratio or credit amount is staged as a literal. Those provisional
calculation passes are not filing packets. The completed parent has both actual
dependent/education claims and source/native checks reconcile MAGI, tax limit,
Schedule3 and Schedule8812 to its actual finalized joint return.

Final child calculation uses actual owned sibling income/deduction returns to
establish line5, then settles reciprocal tax/source projections. Parent export
requires the exact complete actual family inventory; child export recomputes
both sibling and own filed family tax. Joint education export allows the actual
custodial education claimant to be the bound spouse rather than rewriting that
person's tuition/payment identity to the primary stepparent. The existing
primary claimant/refundable review, dependency/source joins and all previous
single-parent cases remain enforced.

Public calculation and native/PDF/complete export conflicts cover wrong first
parent identity, single-versus-joint rate/status, after-credit parent tax,
changed/foreign spouse wage owner or amount, detached joint spouse identity,
wrong custody/remarriage/noncustodial identities, reciprocal sibling tax,
wrong child credit destination, omitted complete family review and wrong
joint MAGI/phaseout/ODC priority. Prior sibling source crossjoin and inventory
cases remain in the selected regression.

remarried custodial parent and stepparent, including stepparent-first identity,
with these two eligible dependent children and issued/service/8r scholarship

custody or award outside the packet or discover omitted family members.
