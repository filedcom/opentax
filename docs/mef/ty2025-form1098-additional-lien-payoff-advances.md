# TY2025 additional mortgage payoff and advance source route

The existing cash-out refinance review includes a complete, retained inventory of mortgages secured by the main and one qualified second home. This addition lets an independently secured inventory lien originate or pay off during 2025, and lets an established open-end lien receive a documented advance. Each lien still has its own printable 2025 Form 1098 Copy B, recorded note, title, monthly lender/payment ledger and, where relevant, contractor evidence.

The [2025 IRS Publication 936, pages 14–16](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/p936--2025.pdf) allows monthly closing balances from lender statements. A single-use lien's sum uses the months that qualified property secured that mortgage. For a mixed-use lien, its example divides each debt category's January–December monthly balances by twelve, including zero months, and applies principal first to personal home-equity debt, next to grandfathered debt, and finally to home-acquisition debt. Table 1 line 12 uses the total balances, including personal debt, and line 14 rounds the single interest-and-points ratio to three decimals. We retain an ambiguity guard when a payment would reach both pre-2017 and post-2017 acquisition categories within one lien; Pub. 936 does not prescribe that vintage allocation.

A newly originated 2025 lien starts on the first day of its first sourced month. A payoff ends on the last day of its final sourced month, with a retained lender receipt matching the final principal payment and statement. An open-end advance requires the recorded credit agreement, limit, dated lender bank disbursement, recipient and property, plus a matching contractor invoice for substantial improvement or a personal-use ledger. Monthly opening, advance, principal paid, closing, interest and unique statement references reconcile exactly to the retained lender ledger and issued box 1. These are reviewed test source records, not authenticated issuer originals.

The independently calculated five-mortgage MFJ examples hold the original cash-out, grandfathered and second-home facts constant. The unchanged before-gap source gives Table 1 ratio .524. A September payoff of the pre-2017 lien yields an additional average of $4,000,000 / 9 and ratio .545. A July 1 new post-2017 lien yields $1,200,000 / 6 and ratio .524. An existing post-2017 lien with a $50,000 September improvement advance, a $20,000 October personal advance, and $10,000 personal-principal payments in each of November and December has total average $2,630,000 / 12, post-2017 qualified average $2,600,000 / 12 and overall ratio .517. The same ratio allocates all issued Forms 1098 box 1 interest and the existing sourced refinance points before Schedule A and Form 1040 are prepared.

Conflicting or missing Copy B, row, retained payoff, note or bank bytes, chronology, property, owner, lender, payment, advancement, use or filed allocation fails the public source parse and both native and direct filled-PDF guards. Additional opening/closing patterns and payments between the two acquisition vintages remain source-dependent and guarded. This route establishes no lender authentication, transmission or IRS acceptance.
