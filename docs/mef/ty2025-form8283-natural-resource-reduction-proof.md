## Proven source route

This extends the existing deduction-below-FMV parent with owned Section175/1252
farmland, legacy nonproducing Section617 exploration, and modern Section1254
oil, gas, geothermal and gold development/exploration property. It supports an
outright gift, not a fictitious taxable sale. Hypothetical ordinary gain reduces
the contribution before the ScheduleA AGI limitation. Current operating cases
derive actual ScheduleC/F income, owner SE, Schedule1 adjustments, itemized
deductions, QBI, tax and carryforwards through the graph.

The source contract retains the purchase/deed or economic-interest account,
every owned annual expense/depletion account through2025, and current
property-use/paid-receipt inventory. Three distinct retained PDFs are joined by
filename, reference and SHA256. Every canonical financial field must match the
source, including actual owner, dated paid invoices and buyer/bank records.
Annual expense invoices identify a C-corporation vendor and its classification
record. These are reproducible simulated source-contract records; they are not
external issuer authentication or verified accepted prior returns.

SectionB continues to require the retained completed official Form8283,
appraisal/declaration and donee facts. The new route does not use reviewer
booleans or synthetic acknowledgments as external acceptance. Source signatures
prove field consistency in a test packet only. Existing conditional
prior-carryover acceptance guards remain intact.

## Authorities and boundaries

- [2025 Form8283 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i8283--2025.pdf):
  apply FMV reductions before the filing threshold, but determine the threshold
  before AGI limits; group similar items across donees; separate SectionB for
  each donee. Similar items to the same donee may share a form. Independently
  completed retained forms also support separate copies with complete item
  facts. Required signatures must accompany the electronic data as completed-PDF
  attachments or Form8453.
- [2025 CFR1.170A-4](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol4/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol4-sec1-170A-4.pdf):
  ordinary-income reduction includes listed recapture, and Section1231(b)
  outright-gift property is deemed capital under paragraph(b)(4). No erroneous
  Section1231(c) loss-lookback condition is added.
- [Section1252](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/pdf/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapP-partIV-sec1252.pdf)
  and
  [2025 Form4797 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i4797--2025.pdf):
  currently applicable soil/water Section175 deductions receive holding-period
  percentages and a hypothetical-gain ceiling. Exact fifth/sixth/ninth
  anniversaries and the following day are tested. The2018 amendment removed
  Section182 land-clearing deductions from current1252.
- [Section1251 repeal](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/pdf/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapP-partIV-sec1250.pdf):
  the former excess-deductions account rule is repealed for post1983 years;
  there is no fabricated2025 positive.
- [Section617](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapI-partI-sec617.htm):
  the retained pre1987 nonproducing exploration route has no depletion
  offset/prior recapture. Paragraph(d)(5) prevents duplicate617 treatment
  where1254 applies.
- [Section1254](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapP-partIV-sec1254.htm),
  [2025 CFR1.1254-1](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1254-1.pdf),
  and
  [gift exception1.1254-2](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title26-vol13/pdf/CFR-2025-title26-vol13-sec1-1254-2.pdf):
  modern deducted drilling/development/exploration costs plus basis-reducing
  depletion determine the ordinary pool; gifts remain subject to170(e), without
  reporting an actual Form4797 sale.
- [2025 Form6251 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i6251): current
  cost depletion and AMT basis/units reconcile. In the paid-IDC case total
  IDC10000 is below65% of net property income30000 (19500), even before any
  normal capital-recovery subtraction. This independently bounds the preference
  at zero; no supplied preference scalar is accepted.

The implemented modern depletion route requires actual cost depletion at least
the uncapped percentage amount, source reserve-unit continuity and no other
basis adjustments. It proves current oil/gas/geothermal cost-depletion income
and bounded zero-IDC preference, not all mining/IDC AMT methods. Producing617
coordination, broader legacy1254 methods, positive mining/IDC AMT
refigures, and broader fractional filed annual-allowance joins remain guarded
existing parent boundaries. This commit does not close the whole Form8283 parent
or claim trusted external authenticity/IRS acceptance.
