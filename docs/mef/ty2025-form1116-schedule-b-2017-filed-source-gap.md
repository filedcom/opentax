# TY2025 Form 1116 Schedule B: 2017 filed-source identity

The passive 2017 vintage already calculates oldest-first use and prints in 2025
Schedule B's eighth-preceding-year column. At full-return native and PDF export,
the retained source now requires a filed 2024 Form 1040 and Schedule B
reference. The filed Schedule B's **seventh-preceding-year** line 8 amount must
equal the retained 2017 vintage, and its line 8 total and passive category must
match the 2025 intake. The filed taxpayer SSN must match the current return. The
Form 1040 and Schedule B document IDs must differ, appear in retained
references, and match the source embedded in the 2025 attachment.

This follows the
[2025 Form 1116 line 10 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1116)
for a 10-year carryforward and the
[Schedule B line 1/4 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1116sb) for
vintage reconciliation and earliest-year use. A positive fixture joins the 2017
source through Form 1116, Schedule 3, Form 1040, native Form 1116 and Schedule
B, and both PDFs. Altered filed amount, owner, and document-identity fixtures
reject at native and PDF export in the deferred bulk validation.

This checks reviewed transcriptions and document IDs, not filed-return bytes.
Pre-2018 general-category allocation, carrybacks, redeterminations, and other
unmodeled filing histories remain outside this bounded route. Full tests,
typecheck, XSD, filled-PDF appearance, and IRS acceptance are pending the shared
bulk validation pass.
