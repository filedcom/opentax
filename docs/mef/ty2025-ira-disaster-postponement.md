# TY2025 IRA rollover under Missouri disaster notice MO-2025-02

The
[IRS disaster relief page](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/disaster-relief-for-retirement-plans-and-iras)
identifies the 60-day rollover deadline as an act that can be postponed for
affected taxpayers.
[Revenue Procedure 2018-58, section 8](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-irbs/irb18-50.pdf)
lists a rollover under section 408(d)(3) among the time-sensitive acts. The
procedure alone does not grant relief; an IRS notice must do so.
[MO-2025-02](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-announces-tax-relief-for-taxpayers-impacted-by-severe-storms-straight-line-winds-tornadoes-and-wildfires-in-missouri-various-deadlines-postponed-to-nov-3)
postpones qualifying acts originally due from March 14 through November 2, 2025,
to November 3, 2025, for affected residents of its listed Missouri counties. It
identifies FEMA declaration 4867-DR. Later county additions need a separately
reviewed notice; they are not inferred.

The bounded positive route accepts an otherwise eligible 2025 traditional/SEP
IRA rollover whose ordinary 60-day deadline falls in that window and whose
deposit is after that deadline but no later than November 3. It requires the
owner SSN on the payer Form 1099-R to match a reviewed March 14 residence record
in one of the 25 counties named in MO-2025-02. The source also retains distinct
residence, IRS notice review, and deposit-confirmation references. Existing
payer-copy, IRA registration, non-inherited, non-RMD, prior-rollover,
source/destination, and plan-acceptance checks still apply. A disaster
postponement cannot be combined with another late waiver or extension. A mere
disaster declaration or unlisted county does not open the route.

The synthetic Butler County case distributes $5,000 on April 1 and deposits it
August 15. Its ordinary deadline is May 31, inside the notice window. Focused
tests verify Form 1040 lines 4a/4b/4c(1), the linked native IRA statement, three
PDF pages and extracted statement text, local TY2025 v5.4 full-return XSD,
deadline and owner rejection, and native/PDF tamper rejection. Internal evidence
identifiers stay in the retained source rather than the transmitted statement or
PDF. The fixture uses reviewed references, not authenticated payer, custodian,
IRS, or residence-document bytes. The release's other affected-taxpayer
categories, subsequently added counties, broader disaster notices, prior-return
evidence, visual PDF review, full-suite tests, and IRS acceptance remain open.
