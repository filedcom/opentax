passive ordinary Form 4797 line 10 gains/losses in investment income, with
exclusions against double counting other worksheet categories. The 2025 limit is
$11,950. [Schedule K-1 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1065sk1)
send code R specially allocated ordinary gains/losses to Form 4797 Part II
line 10. [Form 8582 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8582)
distinguish continuing activities and entire dispositions. This proof uses
current ordinary gains, not a disguised rental or an entire-disposition
assertion.
