`N` means none. `W` means a focused source, validation, MeF, PDF, XSD, or E2E
case was located in the worktree or its gap audit. `X` and `E` identify a
**named** XSD or E2E case, respectively; absence of those letters does not prove
no generic suite covers the form. **Every case is written/unrun in this build
pass.** No current-worktree test, typecheck, XSD, filled-PDF/render, cross-form
E2E, IRS business-rule, or ATS result has been established for any row. Thus
every row's ATS state is **not attempted**, and every unsupported path below
remains open until a sourced filing path and these gates are proven.
