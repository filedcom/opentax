# TY2025 Form 8962: claimed dependent and nonenrolled other taxpayer

One bounded Situation 4 policy can now cover the filer, the filer's claimed
dependent, and a distinct child claimed by another taxpayer who is not enrolled.
The filer must be the 1095-A recipient. The existing reviewed claim packet
identifies the other covered child, other taxpayer, policy, Marketplace
enrollment, tax-family review, and allocation agreement. The filing boundary now
rejects a packet that assigns the filer's own claimed dependent to the other tax
family. The three covered SSNs, recipient, Part IV SSN, required-filing
dependent income records, and one full-year agreed percentage must reconcile.

The source monthly premiums, SLCSP and APTC are allocated at the same
percentage, then Form 8962 uses the two-person household income and poverty
line. Native and PDF export recheck the computed monthly rows and Schedule 3
line 9/Form 1040 line 31 credit or Schedule 2 line 1a/Form 1040 line 17
repayment. A $4,800 net-credit case and $1,200 repayment case, plus changed
other-family claim, allocation, dependent identity, and return-amount fixtures
are authored for the deferred bulk gate. The rule follows
[2025 Form 8962 Situation 4](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8962).

The packet references and hashes are entered review metadata; Marketplace,
claim, and agreement bytes are not independently authenticated. Other household
sizes, multiple policies, mixed situations, changed coverage or allocation
periods, and interstate policies remain outside this bounded route.
