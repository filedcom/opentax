## Source and authority

[2025 Form 8995-A instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8995a) require common ownership of at least 50% for a majority of the year including its last day, the same tax year, no SSTB, and at least two operational factors. [26 CFR 1.199A-4(c)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.199A-4) requires annual disclosure describing each aggregated business, its operating entity name/EIN, current-year formation/acquisition/disposition/cessation, and any RPE aggregation. An individual may not elect aggregation on an amended return; consistency and Commissioner disaggregation boundaries remain applicable.

The supported extension uses actual directly owned regular Schedule C stores, each 100% owned by the same Single filer throughout the required period. Source member counts derive from the complete retained Schedule C inventory, not a fixed two- or three-business maximum. Member business references remain distinct. Distinct businesses of one sole proprietor may share an EIN; each EIN, name, description, and owner still reconciles by business reference to its actual Schedule C.

Each election now retains a complete reviewed annual event inventory, business descriptions and entity identities, actual dated acquisition/formation records, a timely original-return election review and an explicit no-Commissioner-disaggregation review. Missing facts are rejected; absence does not imply no events. Disposed/ceased operations and changed continuing elections remain guarded pending their complete historical/owner route. RPE aggregation remains explicitly guarded: the existing issued K-1 source does not yet retain all RPE aggregated members, reported wages/UBIA, and the required attached issuer statement. Current reviewed records establish joins/calculation evidence; they do not authenticate ownership, annual events, payroll, an issued RPE statement or prior IRS history externally.

The shared return keeps actual Schedule C profits and one settled owner half-SE deduction. Member QBI adjustments sum to the finalized Schedule 1 deductions; grouped wages/UBIA and QBI reconcile to the parent and finalized Form 1040. Existing raw amount and rounding routes, including integrated owned SEP, are unchanged.
