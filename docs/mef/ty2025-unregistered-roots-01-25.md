Static review: 2026-09-28. These are the **first 25 historical `No` rows in order** in the
[211-root TY2025 v5.4 census](ty2025-xsd-document-root-census.md). Some now
have a source literal or registration; historical absence alone neither excludes a situation nor
proves the root is required. The checked-in
`.state/research/docs/IMF_Series_2025v5.4/1040x_Schema_2025v5.4/2025v5.4/IndividualIncomeTax/Ind1040/ReturnData1040.xsd`
supplies each root's description, position, and maximum occurrences. The local
source comparison is against `forms/f1040/2025/inputs.ts`, its named nodes,
`forms/f1040/2025/mef/forms/index.ts`, and the MeF builder. Official IRS links
below establish the stated filing purpose; they do not establish MeF acceptance.

`Applicable; unsupported` means a real individual Form 1040 case can need this
form, while the present exporter has no native root. `Needs decision` means the
attachment, source-only, or workflow treatment is still unresolved. Neither
status is a scope exclusion. `Source-only candidate` is likewise provisional.
The schema `max` column records an XSD ceiling, **not** a rule that a taxpayer
must file that many copies. No tests, typecheck, XSD validation, filled-PDF
render, business-rule check, or ATS transmission ran for this review.
