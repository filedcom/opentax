# TY2025 Form 6251 line 2q: current-year Schedule C mining costs

The [2025 Form 6251 line 2q instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i6251--2025.pdf)
require mining exploration and development costs deducted for regular tax to be
capitalized and amortized over ten years for AMT. Line 2q is the regular
deduction less the AMT deduction. The adjustment does not apply to costs for
which the optional regular-tax ten-year write-off was elected.

A bounded active route now starts with one taxpayer-operated, materially
participated Schedule C business and one named Part V mining expense paid or
incurred in 2025. Its reviewed property workpaper identifies the exact expense
description, property, and source record, affirms that the regular ten-year
election was not made, and excludes property with an unamortized-loss issue.
The expense must be a positive whole-dollar amount divisible by ten. The
calculation subtracts one-tenth from the full regular deduction and puts the
remaining nine-tenths on Form 6251 line 2q. The export guard replays the
retained Schedule C expense and calculated net profit against Schedule 1 line
3, then requires the printed line 2q to match. The normal Form 6251 calculation
carries the adjustment through AMTI, Schedule 2 line 2, and Form 1040 line 17.
The TY2025 IRS6251 XSD names the native field `MiningCostsAmt`; the 2025 PDF
field is page 1 `f1_21`. Positive full-return and source, line, and Schedule 1
tamper fixtures are authored for the deferred validation pass.

The workpaper references and affirmations are reviewed metadata, not
authenticated vendor or taxpayer documents. Earlier-year mining costs,
multiple mining properties or activities, K-1 costs, at-risk or passive
limitations, regular section 59(e) elections, and unamortized property losses
remain closed. Other Form 6251 lines 2p and 2r–2t remain separate gaps.
