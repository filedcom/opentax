## Applicable authority and source contract

[2025 Form1120-S instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1120s),
shareholder pro rata share and ScheduleK-1 itemG, allocate income/loss by shares
held daily; an unchanged full-year interest uses that percentage.
[Form7203 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i7203) require separate
Forms7203 for spouses' K-1s.
[2025 Form8995 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8995), line1(c),
report netQBI per trade/business and exclude basis-suspended current losses;
line16 carries the allowed net qualified loss forward.

The previous distinct-issuer family enforced two different EINs and 100% source
ownership. This repair scopes a source identity to recipient+issuer: one EIN can
legitimately issue different K-1s to the two actual MFJ owners. Their current
debt/basis is still independently limited, with a separate required Form7203 for
each shareholder/corporation pair. A repeated pair or reused issued
K-1/199A/instrument/funding reference rejects.

A shared issuer additionally requires reciprocal identical complete corporate
records: unchanged original stock blocks and payment balances, current issued
shareholder K-1/QBI inventory, actual corporate receipts/costs and one corporate
bank rollforward. Each owner's original $500 cash cost buys100 shares at$5; two
unchanged100-share blocks yield1/2 allocation. The constructed current shared
corporate receipts$12,000/costs$20,000 yield an $8,000 loss and an independently
derived $4,000 issued loss per shareholder. These additional shared corporate
records do not relabel the previous separately owned $6,000/$10,000 accounts or
alter original archives.

Each capital, note advance and principal repayment joins the corresponding
owner's bank/instrument record and shared bank statement reference, date, amount
and actual corporate cash before/after. Operating payments join the full
corporate ordinary account; shared issuer source copies must agree exactly.
Missing owner sources, unproved fractions and detached/interrupted bank
rollforwards cannot be replaced by asserted scalar shares/profit.

More than one corporation per owner requires a complete reciprocal owner-issued
business inventory, precisely matching actual current K-1s. The formerly literal
no-other-business/otherScheduleE assertions become false only with that source
inventory; provided inventories are always reconciled. Single-source exports
cannot drop another co-owned/owner-business source. Joint QBI business rows
combine the same source-proved trade after each owner's basis limit;
owner/corporation current and basis-suspended carry keys remain separate. This
is one trade's two interests, not an elective aggregation of separate
businesses.
