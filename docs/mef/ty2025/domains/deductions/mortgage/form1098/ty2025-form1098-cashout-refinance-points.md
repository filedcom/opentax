# TY2025 cash-out refinance points joined to Form 1098

This bounded route adds ordinary, unreported refinancing points to the existing one-property, old-loan/new-loan cash-out mortgage review. It retains the old purchase, closing and payoff, personal-use disbursements, monthly lender balances and interest, and both printable issued Form 1098 Copy B records. The points source adds the new lender's closing disclosure, the taxpayer's separately paid bank debit, service-fee amount, signed loan-term reference, and each dated monthly payment tied to the lender statement. The payer must be the Form 1098 recipient and the first-of-month settlement must match the new mortgage. The points node consumes a copy of the complete Form 1098 review to derive the Pub. 936 ratio during calculation; both exporters require that source to match the actual filed Form 1098 source exactly. Neither a supplied allocation decimal nor an unattached total can determine the deduction.

[2025 Publication 936, Points and Refinancing](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p936) says ordinary refinance points generally amortize over the loan term, excludes service charges, and applies the Table 1 line 14 decimal to the points calculated under Part I. The [2025 Schedule A instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sca) place points not reported on Form 1098 on line 8c. The current source says no box 6 points; the separately paid points cannot be reclassified as a purchase-year box 6 amount. The monthly term is limited to 30 years and each payment must be dated in the same month and match the new lender's monthly statement. The existing broader payoff, prior-year, home-improvement, other mortgages and MFS routes retain their separate guards.
