# TY2025 pre-2017 second-home acquisition debt beside mixed refinance

The existing Form 1098 parent now distinguishes a second-home purchase mortgage incurred after October 13, 1987 and before December 16, 2017 from the old/new post-2017 main-home refinance. [2025 Publication 936](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p936) Table 1 puts the former average on line 2 and the latter qualified averages on line 7. With no grandfathered debt, line 6 is the lesser of the pre-2017 average and $1 million ($500,000 married filing separately). Lines 9–11 combine that amount with the $750,000 ($375,000 MFS) later-debt threshold. One line 14 ratio limits paid interest on all three mortgages and the current-year refinance points. The pre-2017 treatment is derived only when the second home's actual Form 1098 origination date, retained purchase note purchase/secured dates, documented price, principal, property and acquisition use agree. A stale date, absent classification, or purchase price below principal rejects; no generic older-loan flag bypasses source review.
