# TY2025 conditional-root inventory: AMT, gifts, disaster, and disclosures

Static 2026-09-28 audit of twelve `ReturnData1040.xsd` roots, distinct from the
housing/child/PFIC and installment/passive/business-credit tranches. Source
routes and registered MeF/PDF descriptors are inspected as written, not
validated. A schema root alone does not prove a TY2025 filing obligation, and
the absence of a serializer is not an approved product exclusion. The agreed
full test batch, TY2025 XSD run, filled-PDF review, IRS business-rule check,
and ATS acceptance have not occurred for these paths.
