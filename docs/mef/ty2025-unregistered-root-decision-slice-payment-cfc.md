# TY2025 Form 1040 root decisions: CFC, payment, timber, and payer records

Build-stage applicability review, 2026-09-29. This resolves **classification and
missing evidence**, not implementation, exclusion, or permission to transmit.
The twelve roots below are present in the checked-in TY2025 v5.4
`ReturnData1040.xsd` but have no registered 2025 native MeF and PDF pair.
The [ordered root reviews](ty2025-unregistered-roots-01-25.md) are historical;
the [current census](ty2025-xsd-document-root-census.md), public input registry,
and form registry were rechecked for this slice. No full tests, XSD validation,
filled-PDF review, business-rule run, or ATS transmission was performed.

An **attached** row means the individual can own a filed document in the
stated circumstance. A **source** row means the payer's record supplies facts
for the individual's return; an IRS requirement to attach a copy in a particular
case still needs to be honored. A **payment instruction** is an optional
e-file/e-pay request, not a credit for tax already paid. None of these
classifications is an approved product exclusion.

| Root | Form 1040 trigger and owner | Current local path | Exact unresolved evidence and release boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `IRS1040X` | **Attached amendment workflow, not an original-return schedule.** A taxpayer correcting an already-filed 1040 uses Form 1040-X with changed forms and schedules; [2025 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040x) permit e-filing and require the corrected return package. | No amended-return public input, original-versus-corrected return model, or descriptor. Current `f1040` is an original-return path. | Need authenticated original return and IRS adjustments, year-specific corrected graph, changed-form selection, explanation, payments/refund reconciliation, signature/acknowledgment flow, and TY2025 MeF amendment rules. Decide separate workflow explicitly, not a blanket exclusion; Form 1116 Schedule C can require affected-year amendment evidence. |
| `IRS8992` | **Individual-owned attachment** when the person meets the [U.S.-shareholder/CFC last-day filing test](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8992), including an individual partner of a domestic partnership; an entity's Form 8992 is not automatically the individual's. | Public `f5471` has an asserted `gilti_inclusion` routed to Schedule 1 line 8z. No `f8992` intake, computed GILTI ledger, or native/PDF 8992. | Need ownership dates and section 958(a) share, each CFC's reviewed Schedule I-1/foreign-year results, tested income/loss, QBAI and interest, partnership K-3 where applicable, allocated inclusion, and tie-out to the 1040 income/section 962 tax. An asserted `gilti_inclusion` is not Form 8992. |
| `IRS8992ScheduleA` | **Individual-owned companion** to Form 8992 for a U.S. shareholder outside a U.S. consolidated group; [Schedule A instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8992) require each CFC's pro rata data, including continuation sheets when needed. Schedule B is for a consolidated corporate group. | Same partial `f5471` amount path; no per-CFC Schedule I-1 join, Schedule A descriptor, or continuation source. | Need CFC EIN/reference IDs matching Form 5471, column-by-column tested items, ownership allocation, Part I/II tie-out to 8992, and continuation capacity. Do not emit Schedule A merely because `f5471` contains one positive aggregate amount. |
| `IRS8993` | **Individual-owned attachment** for a [section 962 electing individual](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8993) computing the section 250 deduction; ordinary non-electing individual GILTI does not create this Form 8993 route. The [2025 1040 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040gi) put the section 962 tax on line 16 with a statement, not a stand-alone deduction on line 12e. | `f5471` exposes a GILTI amount but no section 962 election, Form 1118 deemed-paid credit, section 250 computation, 1040 line 16 adjustment, statement, or Form 8993 descriptor. | Need election evidence, Form 8992 and Form 1118 joins, section 78 gross-up, 2025 taxable-income limit and Form 8993 line-by-line calculation, plus line 16 statement and native/PDF output. Do not treat any GILTI amount as an automatic section 250 deduction. |
| `IRS926` | **Individual-owned information attachment** when a U.S. citizen/resident transfers reportable property to a foreign corporation; [IRS Form 926 guidance](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/form-926-filing-requirement-for-us-transferors-of-property-to-a-foreign-corporation) says file it with the income-tax return for the transfer year. A corporation's own transfer is its own return, not the individual's 926. | No `f926` transfer-party/property input or native/PDF descriptor. Generic `f5471` ownership cannot establish a section 6038B transfer. | Need transferor/transferee identity, property and valuation/basis, ownership before/after, transaction dates and recognition/election facts, required section 367 statements and resulting Form 1040 gain basis; only then can a truthful attachment trigger be guarded. |
| `IRSFormT` | **Individual-owned conditional timber attachment**, not a payment. [2025 Schedule C instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sc) require Form T for timber depletion, and [2025 Publication 225](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p225) covers depletion, cutting election, and outright sale. An entity-owned timber operation files its own Form T; the individual's K-1 is source. | No Form T public input or descriptor. The generic Schedule C expense fields do not identify timber units, depletion accounts, or a cutting election. | Need owner and activity, block/account opening basis and units, acquisitions/cuts/sales, section 631 election and gain destination, Schedule C/F/4797/AMT tie-out, and required statement/continuation facts. Remove it from the payment-workflow decision. |
| `IRS1062Payment` | **Optional payment instruction tied to an individual-owned Form 1062 election**, not an ordinary payment record. [2025 Form 1062 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1062) allow eligible farmland-sale tax to be paid in four installments for tax years beginning after July 4, 2025. The checked-in `IRS1062Payment.xsd` contains bank and requested-payment fields. | No Form 1062 election, Schedule A/covenant, installment ledger, direct-debit consent, or payment descriptor. The XSD has `IRS1062Payment`, but the current 1040 root census does not show a parent `IRS1062` root. | Need qualifying sale/farmer and tax-attributable computation, signed election/covenant, due-year installment balance, explicit bank authorization and MeF business-rule/schema decision for Form 1062 and this payment root. Do not mistake it for a paid 2025 estimated-tax credit or infer parent support from the payment root. |
| `IRS965Payment` | **Optional section 965 payment instruction** when a filer with an actual [Form 965-A installment liability](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-965-a) elects an e-file direct debit. It is separate from tax calculation and from a payment already made. | Public `f965` and native Form 965-A calculate/report remaining section 965 liability; its `current_year_payment` is a historical amount with reference. No bank authorization or `IRS965Payment` descriptor. | Need accepted/reconciled 965-A year ledger, amount due versus previously paid, requested date, bank-account consent and MeF payment/business-rule evidence. Never populate a direct-debit request from `current_year_payment` or infer an election from the outstanding liability. |
| `IRSESPayment` | **Optional future estimated-tax direct-debit instruction**, not the 2025 estimated payments claimed on 1040 line 26. [IRS e-file payment guidance](https://www.irs.gov/payments/electronic-funds-withdrawal-for-individuals) permits up to four next-year quarters with a 1040-series return, with no duplicate request on an extension. | Public `f1040es` sums already-made 2025 quarterly payments into line 26. No future-year payment request, bank consent, duplicate-request check, or `IRSESPayment` descriptor. | Need explicit payment-year/quarter, amount and due date, verified bank authorization, 2350/4868 duplicate check, current MeF year/business rules and cancellation/acknowledgment treatment. Do not derive scheduled payments from `f1040es`. |
| `IRSPayment` | **Optional return-balance electronic funds withdrawal**, not a tax form and not an amount already credited. [IRS EFW guidance](https://www.irs.gov/payments/electronic-funds-withdrawal-for-individuals) permits an e-file/e-pay request for a balance due. | Form 1040 computes balance due, but no explicit payment request, bank consent, request date, or `IRSPayment` descriptor. Refund direct-deposit data, if any, is not debit authorization. | Need final accepted balance, chosen debit amount/date, bank-account ownership and authorization, duplicate-payment handling, MeF rules, acknowledgment/cancellation state, and secure payment-data handling. No automatic debit based on line 37. |
| `IRSRRB1042S` | **Payer-issued source with conditional copy-attachment review.** [2025 Publication 915](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p915) describes nonresident-alien RRB statements and corrected copies; a later resident-status or refund claim can reach Form 1040. It is not taxpayer-authored. | The public registry has `ssa1099` and railroad distribution paths, not a typed RRB-1042S statement with withholding/status periods or a native/PDF copy route. | Need status dates, issued/corrected copy, SSEB versus pension component, treaty/withholding amount and 1040 income/credit destination. Determine original-return copy attachment under TY2025 MeF rules; amended-return [instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040x) explicitly require a supporting RRB-1042S copy. Do not synthesize a payer statement from numeric income. |
| `IRSSSA1042S` | **Payer-issued source with a proven narrow 1040 copy requirement.** [2025 Publication 915](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p915) says a lawful permanent resident seeking refund of erroneous SSA withholding may need a 1040/1040-SR with the SSA-1042S copy, green-card evidence and signed declaration. Otherwise it chiefly reports nonresident payments, outside the agreed standalone 1040-NR scope. | `ssa1099` is a different statement type; there is no SSA-1042S input, withholding/status ledger, source-copy attachment, or related declaration path. | Need original/corrected SSA statement, residency and withholding dates, refund-at-SSA status, green-card/declaration evidence, 1040 income and withholding reconciliation, and an IRS-approved electronic attachment representation. Do not recast this as SSA-1099 or claim the narrow resident-refund path is excluded. |

The four payment roots require **separate explicit user authorization for a debit**;
the current tax-return calculator's amounts cannot stand in for that consent.
`IRSFormT` belongs with conditional business attachments. The CFC trio
(`IRS8992`, its Schedule A, and `IRS8993`) and `IRS926` are individual-owned
filing paths when their facts apply, even if related entity documents are
sources. For these twelve roots, the product decision is still open and no
positive filing support is claimed.
