# TY2025 registered Form 1040 wage and supporting documents

Static evidence snapshot, 2026-09-28. These **14** registered descriptors are
the support tranche outside the separately assigned 965-A–4952 statements and
the later-numbered parent-form audit. They are source/statement documents, not
14 additional taxpayer-computed tax forms. The other four later supporting roots
(`AnyOtherTaxesStatement`, the Form 8814 child-interest statement, the Form 8820
controlled-group statement and Form 8854 native statements) are tracked with
their parent forms in the later-numbered tranche.

All names below are registered in `ALL_MEF_FORMS`; no full-batch test, current
local XSD validation, filled-PDF render, IRS business-rule verification, or ATS
acceptance was run for this pass. A named case is written evidence only. A
missing dedicated case is an open evidence gap, not a test failure or approval
to omit the document. None of these roots is an approved exclusion.
