# TY2025 filled-PDF first review, 2026-09-29

This is a first visual pass over thirteen **synthetic** source returns. It does
not close the product board's per-route PDF or source-to-native-XML gates.
The final batch was generated by
`deno run --allow-read --allow-write --allow-net=www.irs.gov scripts/generate-ty2025-pdf-review.ts /tmp/pdfs/ty2025-board-review-iter31`.
The PDFs, source/pending JSON, native XML, page counts, and SHA-256 manifest are
retained locally at `.state/research/ty2025-filled-pdf-review/2026-09-29-v6/`. The
first eleven cases were rendered to contact sheets; selected discrepancy pages
and the new Form 8615 and Form 8960 pages were rerendered at higher resolution. All thirteen
native returns built from those same source fixtures validated against TY2025
v5.4 `Return1040.xsd`. The final batch's twelve prior cases (61 pages) are
pixel-identical at 65 dpi to the previously reviewed batch. The new
high-wage case adds six pages.
`deno test --allow-read --allow-write --allow-run=xmllint,deno
forms/f1040/2025/pdf/review-fixtures.xsd.test.ts` passed 13/13 with the local
schema bundle. This verifies executor diagnostics are empty and native XML is
structurally valid for each retained source fixture.

Discrepancies found while getting the batch to render:

- Context-only Forms 461, 2210-F, 4952, 7206, 8829, and Schedule 1-A could
  trigger or block unrelated PDF exports. Their absence handling was corrected.
- Schedule C sent a dashed EIN to a nine-character IRS comb field. The PDF now
  prints the nine digits.
- Schedule E PDF refused a suspended rental loss even though native Schedule E
  had a linked Form 8582 allocation. The projector now uses that same allocation
  for lines 22, 25, and 26, and rejects a mismatched activity.
- Schedule 1 and Schedule 2 page-one filer identity boxes were blank. Their
  canonical name and SSN fields are now mapped; the rerendered Schedule 1 page
  shows both values.
- The Form 1116 PDF guard treated context-only Schedule 1-A data as a claim.
  It now checks the actual additional-deduction claim and review flag.
- The new child's XML initially contained an `IRS8960` document with taxable
  interest but no NIIT, while the PDF bundle omitted Form 8960. The MeF pending
  adapter now excludes the source-only intermediate slice; the regenerated XML
  has no `IRS8960`, and a regression assertion covers this case. The
  [IRS 2025 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8960) require Form
  8960 when modified AGI exceeds the applicable threshold. The new wage-only
  case confirms a zero-tax Form 8960 is emitted above that threshold in both
  XML and PDF, without a Schedule 2 NIIT amount.
- Form 8959 PDF projection initially parsed finalized source and computed fields
  as though they were all print fields and rejected the W-2 case. It now
  validates the calculated print subset and still reconciles it against the
  original W-2 deposit. Forms 8959 and 8960 initially omitted their name/SSN
  boxes; those fields are now mapped.

The contact sheets establish page presence and broad placement, not detailed
approval of every checkbox, amount, continuation, or descriptor. Apart from the
Form 8615 and Form 8960 page cross-checks above, each case still needs line-by-line comparison
of its PDF with the retained JSON source,
finalized pending graph, native XML, and official form. The thirteen cases also
cover only a subset of the 86 registered PDF descriptors. Structural XSD
success does not establish IRS business-rule or ATS acceptance.
