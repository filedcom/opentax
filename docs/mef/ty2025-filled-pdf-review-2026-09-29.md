# TY2025 filled-PDF first review, 2026-09-29

This is a first visual pass over eleven **synthetic** source returns. It does
not close the product board's per-route PDF or source-to-native-XML gates.
The final batch was generated by
`deno run --allow-read --allow-write --allow-net=www.irs.gov scripts/generate-ty2025-pdf-review.ts /tmp/pdfs/ty2025-board-review-iter18`.
The PDFs, source/pending JSON, page counts, and SHA-256 manifest are retained
locally at `.state/research/ty2025-filled-pdf-review/2026-09-29-v2/`. Every
page was rendered to a contact sheet; selected discrepancy pages were
rerendered at higher resolution. The final batch's 57 pages are pixel-identical
at 65 dpi to the first reviewed batch; the rerun added mailing-address source
facts needed for MeF without changing the printable pages.

Discrepancies found while getting the batch to render:

- Context-only Forms 461, 2210-F, 4952, 7206, 8829, and Schedule 1-A could
  trigger or block unrelated PDF exports. Their absence handling was corrected.
- Schedule C sent a dashed EIN to a nine-character IRS comb field. The PDF now
  prints the nine digits.
- Schedule E PDF refused a suspended rental loss even though native Schedule E
  had a linked Form 8582 allocation. The projector now uses that same allocation
  for lines 22, 25, and 26, and rejects a mismatched activity.
- Schedule 1 and Schedule 2 page-one filer identity boxes were blank. Their
  canonical name and SSN fields are now mapped; the rerendered Schedule 1 page
  shows both values.
- The Form 1116 PDF guard treated context-only Schedule 1-A data as a claim.
  It now checks the actual additional-deduction claim and review flag.

The contact sheets establish page presence and broad placement, not detailed
approval of every checkbox, amount, continuation, or descriptor. Each case
still needs line-by-line comparison with its JSON source, finalized pending
graph, native XML, and official form. The eleven cases also cover only a subset
of the 86 registered PDF descriptors.
