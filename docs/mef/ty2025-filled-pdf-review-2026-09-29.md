Discrepancies found while getting the batch to render:

- Context-only Forms 461, 2210-F, 4952, 7206, 8829, and Schedule 1-A could
  trigger or block unrelated PDF exports. Their absence handling was corrected.
- Schedule C sent a dashed EIN to a nine-character IRS comb field. The PDF now
  prints the nine digits.
- Schedule E PDF refused a suspended rental loss even though native Schedule E
  had a linked Form 8582 allocation. The projector now uses that same allocation
  for lines 22, 25, and 26, and rejects a mismatched activity.
- Schedule 1 and Schedule 2 page-one filer identity boxes were blank. Their
  canonical name and SSN fields are now mapped; the rerendered Schedule 1 page
  shows both values.
- The Form 1116 PDF guard treated context-only Schedule 1-A data as a claim.
  It now checks the actual additional-deduction claim and review flag.
- The new child's XML initially contained an `IRS8960` document with taxable
  interest but no NIIT, while the PDF bundle omitted Form 8960. The MeF pending
  adapter now excludes the source-only intermediate slice; the regenerated XML
  has no `IRS8960`, and a regression assertion covers this case. The
  [IRS 2025 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8960) require Form
  8960 when modified AGI exceeds the applicable threshold. The new wage-only
  case confirms a zero-tax Form 8960 is emitted above that threshold in both
  XML and PDF, without a Schedule 2 NIIT amount.
- Form 8959 PDF projection initially parsed finalized source and computed fields
  as though they were all print fields and rejected the W-2 case. It now
  validates the calculated print subset and still reconciles it against the
  original W-2 deposit. Forms 8959 and 8960 initially omitted their name/SSN
  boxes; those fields are now mapped.
