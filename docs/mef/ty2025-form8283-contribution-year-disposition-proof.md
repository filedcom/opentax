## Official rule and calculation

The [2025 Form8283 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i8283--2025.pdf)
and [2025 Pub526](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/p526--2025.pdf) require removal
of appreciation for tangible personal property valued above$5,000 when the donee
disposes of it in the contribution year without the prescribed exempt-use
certification. This source route is an outright original-donee sale of purchased,
long-term, nondepreciable personal property to a50%-limit organization. It retains
purchase, original donor/donee/property, receipt/sale/proceeds, donor-copy notice,
and the reviewed certification inventory. The deduction equals adjusted basis;
nonvehicle sale proceeds do not replace that basis claim. No actual taxable sale
by the donor or Schedule1 recapture income is fabricated.

The original Form8283 donee answer remains **No** to intended unrelated use.
The donee subsequently chooses a fundraising sale without actual related use or
an impossibility certificate. Its completed donor copy of the official
[Form8282 October2021 revision](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/f8282--2021.pdf)
answers entire-interest **Yes**, exempt-use **No**, leaves PartIV certification
blank, and completes the separate ordinary report declaration. A source inventory
records absence of both statutory certificates. The electronic field guard binds
actual AcroForm donor/donee identity/address, property/use narratives, dates,
proceeds, use/interest answers and officer name/title; provided conflicting fields
reject even after their byte/source/attachment inventories are rehashed.

The source-derived disposition classification has its own ScheduleA confirmation;
it does not change the original intended-use answer. The filed amount is reduced
before determining SectionA/B thresholds and before ScheduleA's AGI limit.
Grouping covers all donees; separate signed-source references remain distinct
native/PDF copies. SectionA retains its actual3 source PDFs; SectionB additionally
retains full qualified appraisal, signed8283, separate computation and signatures.
