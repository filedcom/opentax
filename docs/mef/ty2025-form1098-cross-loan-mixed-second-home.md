# TY2025 mixed cash-out refinance with a separate second-home mortgage

[2025 Publication 936](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p936) requires one Table 1 across a taxpayer's main and qualified second home, regardless of how many mortgages secure them. Its line 7 includes all post-2017 home acquisition balances; line 12 includes all mortgage balances, including the personal category in a mixed-use loan; line 14 yields one three-decimal ratio for paid interest. The same ratio limits current-year refinance points. This route extends the existing old/new main-home refinance to a separately secured, pure acquisition-debt second-home loan. The old lender's months-secured balance, new mixed loan's twelve-month category balance and second home's twelve monthly lender balances are separately traced before aggregation. Personal cash-out principal is paid down first.
