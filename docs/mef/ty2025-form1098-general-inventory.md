# Form 1098: source-backed mortgage inventory across two homes

This slice extends the existing cash-out refinance review to a complete entered inventory of two main-home refinance copies and any number of additional *separately sourced* 2025 liens on the main home and one qualifying second home. The retained inventory record must enumerate every entered payer copy exactly once. A second-home lien needs title, 2025 personal-use calendar, lender note, 12 dated balance and interest rows, and a payer ledger. Main-home and second-home ownership and payment must agree with the filed taxpayer or MFJ spouse; MFS cannot pool a nonfiling spouse's lien. Original loans incurred on or before October 13, 1987 need a contemporaneous secured note and a byte-bound continuous-security record through 2025. Later improvement liens need a dated secured note and matched contractor invoice and bank payment for the entire original proceeds. Unproven dates, property use, payments, or lien categories reject at public input and at the native and filled-PDF export.

[IRS Publication 936 (2025), Table 1](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/p936--2025.pdf) calls for one worksheet across the main and second homes, regardless of mortgage count. The source calculator now separately sums average grandfathered debt (line 1), acquisition debt incurred after October 13, 1987 and before December 16, 2017 (line 2), acquisition debt incurred after December 15, 2017 (line 7), and all mortgage balances (line 12). It applies the exact line 4/6/9/11 limit and rounds the single line 14 ratio to three decimals before allocating one combined interest deduction to each issued Form 1098 copy. The existing mixed refinance still applies principal repayments first to personal cash-out and divides its category balances by 12. Ordinary refinance points use the same final ratio. The retained older three-copy and two-copy inputs and outputs are unchanged.
