## Primary authority and calculation contract

[2025 Publication 560](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p560), Chapters 2 and 5, supplies the SEP eligibility, contribution, deadline and self-employed worksheet rules. The written annual employer rate is at most 25%. The self-employed reduced rate is `r/(1+r)` applied to actual business profit after its owner's filed deductible half of SE tax. The owner limit also respects `$350,000 × r` and the TY2025 $70,000 annual cap. Employee contributions follow that same employer rate and compensation/annual limits; their Schedule F pension expense reduces business profit before the owner calculation. The owner's contribution is a Schedule 1 line 16 adjustment, not another Schedule F pension expense.

The complete employee census records DOB, each prior service year, compensation, retained W-2 identity/reference, employment history reference, participant information delivery and actual custodian contribution/payment records. The route includes every census worker meeting the written plan's age/service/compensation requirements. The maximum permitted requirements are age 21, work in any part of three of the preceding five years and $750 TY2025 compensation; less restrictive requirements are accepted. Actual W-2 box 1 compensation must be confirmed as the full plan compensation definition and reconciled to the retained payroll records. Other compensation definitions, excluded worker classes and additional plans need their own source route.

[26 CFR 1.414(c)-4(b)(5)(ii)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.414(c)-4) supplies the four spousal attribution exception conditions. Each actual business requires reviewed no direct cross-ownership at any time, no employment/director/fiduciary/management role at any time, the actual section 61 gross income and classified royalties/rents/dividends/interest/annuities share not exceeding 50%, and no disposal restriction favoring the spouse or children **under 21**. Gross income is reconciled to actual Schedule F books. The remaining ownership/family attribution and affiliated-service-group review is explicit. These reviewed input records do not authenticate plan adoption, payroll, custody, payment, an extension, income classification or employer-group status externally. A controlled or affiliated group is guarded pending its complete employee/allocation route.

[2025 Form 7206 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i7206) join the actual establishing business and owner deductions: its own SEP deduction reduces line 9, then limits health deduction on that business's own earnings. [2025 Form 8995-A instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8995a) require both attributable owner adjustments to reduce that business's QBI before its own qualified-payment allocation, patron reduction and shared return income limits. [2025 Form 1040 whole-dollar instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040gi) require cents to be retained when adding amounts for one line, then rounding only the total. SEP owner deduction worksheets are not separate filed forms: Schedule 1 line 16 rounds the sum of allowable raw owner deductions. The smaller actual contribution / worksheet limit remains in raw deduction provenance; it can differ slightly from actual cash where a cent-rounded payment is just above the fractional limit. A separately filed owner Form 7206 rounds its own line 9. QBI instead subtracts that business's allowable raw SEP deduction before rounding its final Form 8995-A business line 2, preserving the accepted 20% / exact 50-cent boundary. Schedule D retains its existing documented qualified-payment allocation of attributable QBI before its own rounding; its instructions do not require allocation from the rounded parent QBI line 2. Independent rounding can make combined owner QBI and return AGI differ by $1. Schedule 1 and Form 1040 include the raw-summed SEP deduction once.
