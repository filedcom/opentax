# TY2025 unregistered-root applicability crosswalk

Build-stage triage reconciled 2026-10-03. The source is the locally cached, ignored
TY2025 v5.4 `ReturnData1040.xsd` document-root census in
[`ty2025-xsd-document-root-census.md`](ty2025-xsd-document-root-census.md),
compared with public inputs in `forms/f1040/2025/inputs.ts`, the node registry,
and `ALL_MEF_FORMS`. The IRS schema files are not tracked in Git;
reproducible artifact provenance remains open. The historical unregistered-root review is grouped below;
the current census has 83 `No` rows. Of the 128 `Yes` rows, 117 have a literal
in a directly registered descriptor file. Six (`IRS2210`, `IRS8828`,
`IRS8908`, `IRS8938`, `IRS8997`, `IRS9465`) occur only in staged descriptor
files. Five (`IRS3115`, `IRS5471ScheduleO`, `IRS8594`, `IRS8833`, `IRS8883`)
occur only as permitted Form 5471 reference names in the linkage helper;
they are not new builders or verified filing routes. `IRS1116ScheduleC` is
still a seventh staged root builder, with its token also quoted in the
registered Form 1116 parent. Source-literal flags do not resolve applicability.
This file also retains historical review rows for roots registered after that
review. Schedules 1-A, J, LEP and R, Forms 2210-F, 8915-F,
2439/8888, W-2G, and Form 8995-A Schedules C/D are now-registered status notes,
not missing native roots. This is a **disposition queue**, not a completed
form-by-form coverage inventory. Neither a root's presence in the schema nor a
public input proves that it must be attached to every return. Conversely,
absence of a public input is not a user-approved exclusion.

### Registry-to-root reconciliation

The current native registry has **152 descriptor entries**, including 35
entries in its wage/supporting sequence segment. The PDF registry has **118
entries**. The distinct native/PDF pending-key counts are 148/115. These are
descriptor counts, not distinct schema-root counts or accepted filing scenarios.
The 211-root census has 128 `Yes` and 83 `No`: 117 literal roots occur directly
in registered descriptor files, six in staged-only files and five only as
permitted Form5471 reference names in its linkage helper. The separately staged
`IRS1116ScheduleC` builder is also quoted by its registered parent. See the
[census classifications](ty2025-xsd-document-root-census.md) for exact names.
A registered file or helper can mention a root it does not emit.
The [supporting-descriptor review](ty2025-form1040-form-audit.md#supporting-descriptor-case-and-packet-disposition)
records source/parent triggers, written cases and packet roles. Payer copies,
native supporting statements, PDF BinaryAttachments and taxpayer forms are
different artifacts; registration or a schema name does not establish support.

The evidence resolves **owner class**, not every optional filing trigger:
`IRSW2` and `IRSW2G` are payer-issued records, the farm/foreign-wage and
credit/deduction statements are return-linked native XML, and the payment
roots are requested debit instructions. `IRSFormT` is a proprietor timber
attachment, not a payment instruction. The [entity-issued review](ty2025-entity-issued-root-disposition.md)
establishes `IRS1065ScheduleD` and `IRS8825` as other-filer forms for a K-1-only
individual and the conditional `IRS1041ScheduleK1` box-13-code-B attachment.
The [CFC/payment review](ty2025-unregistered-root-decision-slice-payment-cfc.md)
separates individual-owned Form 8992/926 paths from payment requests and
issuer records. Whether to offer optional payment, preference and amendment
workflows, and whether to accept unsupported individual-owner/entity roles,
remains a **user-dependent product decision**. No such decision is applied here.

The received trust/estate K-1 stays source-only for ordinary beneficiary
items. Its newly typed box 13 code B backup-withholding amount is retained but
blocks both exports until Form 1040 line 25c and the required issued K-1 copy
can be reconciled. The `IRS1041ScheduleK1` root remains unregistered; see the
[entity-issued disposition](ty2025-entity-issued-root-disposition.md).

The only currently agreed exclusions are standalone 1040-NR, 1040-SS, Form 4868,
and dual-status 1040 e-file. No additional path below is treated as excluded.
The conditional filing triggers and local gaps for Form 8995-A Schedules A-D and
Form 1116 Schedule C are audited separately in
[`ty2025-conditional-schedule-applicability.md`](ty2025-conditional-schedule-applicability.md).

`Applicable` means a real Form 1040 tax or filing situation can use the root and
needs implementation or an explicit fail-closed disposition. `Undecided` means
the source and required-attachment rules still need review.
`Likely separate-entity/administrative` is a proposed product boundary, **not**
permission to drop it. The team needs to confirm whether it must be attached to
an individual return, consumed only as source evidence, or handled in a separate
workflow.

## Special census caveat

`IRS8839` was `No` in the earlier static source-literal census because its
registered descriptor previously failed closed. A bounded one-child reviewed
claim now has a two-pass calculation and prepared MeF/PDF bundle route with
byte-bound attachments through both programmatic and strict stored-input CLI
entrypoints. Direct synchronous XML and standalone PDF without those bytes
remain closed; exclusion, wider credit ordering, issuer authenticity, and
artifact validation remain open. It is not an unregistered descriptor or
whole-form support.
Similarly, a literal mention of `IRS8886` in a different descriptor does not
create a registered Form 8886 document. Registration must be checked against
`ALL_MEF_FORMS`, and all applicability classifications above remain subject to
the full source-to-filed-return inventory.
