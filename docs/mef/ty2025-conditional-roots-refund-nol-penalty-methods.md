# TY2025 conditional-root inventory: refund, NOL, penalty, and method changes

Static 2026-09-28 audit of twelve `ReturnData1040.xsd` roots. This is a
fourth, nonoverlapping inventory tranche, not an approved exclusion list or
filing-readiness statement. Current source nodes and the registered MeF/PDF
descriptor lists were inspected. Written cases are not a current pass. The
agreed full test batch, TY2025 XSD validation, filled-PDF review, IRS business
rules, and ATS acceptances remain pending.

| Native root | Individual 1040 trigger and source | Current public source, native, and PDF route | Exact unsupported branch or next evidence |

| `IRS1310` | A person claiming a deceased taxpayer's refund can need Form 1310; a surviving spouse on a joint original return and a court-appointed representative with appointment evidence can use exceptions, while amended/reissued-check claims have distinct rules. [2025 form](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/f1310--2025.pdf) and [Pub. 559](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p559). | Public `f1310` stores deceased/claimant assertions but emits no tax or attachment output. Any populated source blocks both exporters; no native or PDF descriptor. | Reconcile deceased indicator/identity, actual Form 1040 refund, original versus amended/reissue claim, claimant authority, signatures and separate court certificate. Do not infer that every decedent return needs this form or that a claimant flag proves the exception. |
| `IRS172` | An individual carrying a net operating loss needs a vintage-by-vintage Form 172 computation, with 2025 regular-tax deduction and potentially distinct AMT NOL treatment; the filed loss-year return and intervening returns are source records. [Form 172 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i172). | Public `nol_carryforward` is a loose asserted balance. No native/PDF Form 172 and no mapped Schedule 1 line 8a. | Build filed-history ledger and 80% limit, source current taxable income and deduction, derive Schedule 1 line 8a and AMT line 2f, then attach one required native Form 172 per relevant loss. | `IRS2106` | Qualifying reservists, fee-basis state/local officials and performing artists can claim Schedule 1 line 12 employee expenses; impairment-related work expenses use Schedule A line 16. | `IRS2120` | A multiple-support agreement for a qualifying **relative** requires the declaration with the 1040; other eligible supporters' signed waivers are retained evidence. Any populated source blocks both exporters. | Prove support shares, eligible supporters, waiver signatures and exact dependent claim; make the declaration and return agree. An empty object cannot establish a legally complete filing. |
| `IRS2210` | The IRS normally computes an underpayment penalty without an attached Form 2210. Certain Part II boxes/waivers require page 1 or a complete form; annualized income needs Schedule AI. A line-38 number alone neither proves nor cures a form-attachment duty; box-E source is not authenticated. |
| `IRS2210F` | A farmer/fisher with at least two-thirds qualifying 2024 or 2025 gross income can use Form 2210-F; filing/penalty depends on required installment, prior-year tax and payment timing. Form 2210 and 2210-F are mutually rejected in the native path. |
| `IRS2439` | A RIC/REIT issues Form 2439 for undistributed long-term gain; box 1a reaches Schedule D and box 2 tax reaches Schedule 3 line 13a. [IRS form](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f2439.pdf) and [Schedule D instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sd). | Public `f2439` has bounded payer Copy B, shareholder and TY2025 period facts; native per-copy descriptor links line 13a and a Copy B PDF descriptor is registered. | Positive box 1c still rejects. | `IRS3115` | Automatic accounting-method changes generally attach the application to the timely year-of-change return **and** send a signed duplicate separately; nonautomatic requests use a separate consent procedure. No native/PDF Form 3115 or consent-agreement evidence. | Determine automatic versus nonautomatic, correct DCN/year of change, business destination, signed consent and section 481 spread; build attached form and track separate duplicate/consent workflow. An income amount alone is not the application. |
| `IRS3903` | An active-duty military permanent-change-of-station move can permit a Schedule 1 line 14 deduction; each move needs its own Form 3903, and excess reimbursements can instead be Form 1040 line 1h income. | `IRS4137` | An employee with unreported or allocated tips can owe employee Social Security/Medicare tax; employer, monthly tips, W-2 allocated tips and wage-base records are sources. | `IRS4547` | An authorized individual may make an optional child's account/pilot-contribution election with a current-year e-filed return or by separate paper/later account channel; it does not attach to 1040-X. | `IRS461` | Noncorporate taxpayers file Form 461 when net trade/business losses exceed $313,000 ($626,000 joint) or any one specified form line has a loss over $156,500; at-risk and passive limits apply first.
