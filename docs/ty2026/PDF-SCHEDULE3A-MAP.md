# TY2026 draft Schedule 3-A PDF field map

Source: pinned [`corpus/draft/f1040s3a.pdf`](corpus/draft/f1040s3a.pdf).
The printed schedule is physical PDF page 2; page 1 is a draft cover. Its
widgets and tooltips are in `pdf-fields-f1040s3a.csv`, generated by
`build_pdf_fields.py`.

The draft has **16 page widgets but zero entries in `/AcroForm/Fields`**.
The `pypdf` reader and page annotation inspection agree on this state. A
normal `pdf-lib` `getForm().getTextField(...)` call cannot find these fields.
`forms/f1040/2026/pdf/schedule3a.ts` therefore uses a static overlay at the
pinned rectangles, then removes the orphaned widgets. The completed TY2025
return bundle is also flattened. The generated one-page Schedule 3-A was
rendered and visually checked; its header, amounts, and line 6/7 checkboxes
are legible. Recheck the final IRS PDF; its field tree may differ from this
draft.

24a, 31, 32a, and 32b. A three-page 1040 plus Schedule 3-A sample passed text
extraction and visual review. Other attached forms remain outside this core
