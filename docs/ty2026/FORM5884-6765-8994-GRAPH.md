# TY2026 work opportunity, research and paid-leave credit sources

Snapshot: September 27, 2026. These three TY2025 input nodes currently send
source credit amounts straight to Schedule 3. For TY2026, each credit must
have its own source record, source form or documented pass-through exception,
then [Form 3800](GENERAL-BUSINESS-CREDIT-GRAPH.md) and Schedule 3 line 6a.
None is registered as a complete 2026 filing route.

## Form 5884 — work opportunity credit

The IRS currently serves [Form 5884 (March 2021)](corpus/authorities/f5884--2021.pdf)
and [instructions](corpus/authorities/i5884--2021.pdf); the form has
[13 PDF fields](pdf-fields-f5884.csv). The IRS [termination
notice](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/form-8850-is-no-longer-in-use) says
the credit does not apply to **employees who begin work after December 31,
2025**. Thus a TY2026 wage payment can still concern a qualifying pre-2026
hire; a 2026 hire cannot create a new source credit under current law.

- Keep employee start date, certification/target group, first- or second-year
  status, hours, qualified wages, veteran/LTFA cap and prior use of those
  wages. Verify Form 8850/agency certification and reduction of the wage
  deduction. Compute the printed lines, then Form 3800 source and
  carryforward rather than crediting Schedule 3 directly.
- The shared [`f5884` node](../../forms/f1040/nodes/inputs/f5884/index.ts)
  has no hire date or certification fact, computes from rate/cap constants,
  and emits `line6a_general_business_credit` directly. Add the date gate,
  source identity and current attachment decision. Test December 31/January
  1 hires, second-year 2026 wages for a 2025 hire, sub-120/120/400 hours,
  target-group limits and pass-through-only credit.

## Form 6765 — increasing research activities credit

The current [Form 6765 (December 2024)](corpus/authorities/f6765--2024.pdf)
and [December 2025 instructions](corpus/authorities/i6765--2025.pdf) are
pinned; [270 PDF fields](pdf-fields-f6765.csv) cover Sections A–G.
The instruction cover calls its target a January 2025 revision while the
currently served form cover says December 2024; compare their printed lines
and recheck the product page before final filing.
For tax years beginning after 2025, the instructions make **Section G
required** unless the qualified small business payroll-election exception
applies, or controlled-group QREs are at most $1.5 million, three-year
average gross receipts at most $50 million, and the credit is on an
original return. A required Section G reports at least 80% of QREs by
business component, at most 50 components, plus aggregate remainder.

- Preserve method election (regular or ASC), §280C reduced-credit election,
  controlled-group allocation, base-period facts, current/three-prior-year
  QREs, Section F categories, Section G business components and attachment
  explanations. Recompute each printed section and reconcile Section G/F
  totals to the source credit. Keep a payroll-tax election separate from
  the income-tax Form 3800 amount and from employment-tax filing.
- The shared [`f6765` node](../../forms/f1040/nodes/inputs/f6765/index.ts)
  accepts a precomputed base amount, omits the printed 50%-of-QRE regular
  cap, special ASC no-prior-QRE branch, §280C election, controlled group and
  Sections F/G. It silently subtracts a payroll election and sends the
  remainder straight to Schedule 3. Replace this with form-line records,
  validated elections, Form 3800 handoff and an explicit employment-tax
  election output. Build PDF/MeF and required statements. Test Section G
  threshold/exception boundaries, 80%/top-50, zero prior QRE, reduced-credit
  election, controlled group and $500,000 payroll election cap.

## Form 8994 — paid family and medical leave credit

The current [Form 8994 (January 2021)](corpus/authorities/f8994--2021.pdf)
and [December 2024 instructions](corpus/authorities/i8994--2024.pdf) have
[13 PDF fields](pdf-fields-f8994.csv), but predate the 2026 changes. Pinned
[Notice 2026-28](corpus/authorities/n26-28--2026.pdf) says §45S is permanent
and permits an employer to elect a **wage or qualifying insurance-premium
method**. It also changes employee/aggregation criteria and prevents claiming
both methods for the same leave. The 2021 PDF is a comparator until a form
revision or filing instruction expresses the 2026 premium method.

- Model written policy, qualifying employees and coverage, customary hours,
  service-duration election, leave and replacement rate, state/local leave,
  wages, premiums by creditable/noncreditable coverage, employer group and
  deduction reduction. Keep leave instance IDs so wage and premium credits
  cannot overlap. Apply the Notice's allocation for mixed insurance and
  reconcile credit to Form 3800 and the underlying wage/premium deduction.
- The shared [`f8994` node](../../forms/f1040/nodes/inputs/f8994/index.ts)
  contains only employee wages/replacement percentages. It lacks policy,
  premium, employee and group eligibility, deduplication, deduction
  adjustment and a Form 3800 route. Obtain current 2026 Form 8994/MeF
  publication before final PDF/XML work. Test wage-only, premium-only,
  mixed coverage, same-leave overlap, part-time/service boundaries and
  controlled-group cases.
