# TY2026 business credit and energy deduction graph

The [ATS scenario 12 packet](ATS-SCENARIO-12.md) exercises two distinct
routes. A §45X credit goes through Form 7207 → Form 3800 Part III/II →
Schedule 3 line 6a → Form 1040 line 20. A §179D deduction goes through Form
7205 and its certification/allocation/PWA evidence → the owner's Schedule C
line 27a → Schedule 1/SE/QBI/1040. Form 4562-B amortization separately feeds
Schedule C line 27b. These routes share the business/activity identity but
must retain different authority, limits, and attachments.

## Current code boundary

- `forms/f1040/2026/registry.ts` has no Schedule C/SE, Form 3800, 7207,
  7205, or 7220 registration. Its Schedule 3 node can receive a general
  business credit amount, but has no TY2026 Form 3800 producer.
- Shared `nodes/inputs/f7207/index.ts` has TY2025 fixed component rates and
  a component-total input only. It bypasses Form 3800 and sends the credit
  directly to TY2025 Schedule 3 line 6a. It cannot represent this packet's
  facility, registration, production year, transferee, or signed election.
- Shared `nodes/inputs/f3800/index.ts` has a generic credit-amount path and
  TY2025 Form 1040/Form 6251 and Schedule 3 destinations. Its own comment
  says an unbounded generic amount cannot represent a filed calculation.
  Review reusable calculation math against the **2026** form, but do not
  register that existing path as a TY2026 filed Form 3800.
- No Form 7205 or 7220 calculation/source node or TY2026 PDF/MeF builder is
  present. Existing TY2025 Form 7205 validation rules are only review leads.
  TY2025 MeF has Form 3800, but no 7205/7207/7220/3800 Schedule A serializer.

## Data ownership and calculation order

1. Give each business activity, building/project, facility, component batch,
   and transfer election a stable ID. Store the primary documents with their
   owner/TIN, tax period, registration, dates, and attachment status. A
   transfer-in record names the transferor and transferee; an earned-credit
   record names its producer. Scenario 12 must fail reconciliation if both
   records claim the same credit as separate benefits.
2. Calculate Form 7207 component rows from qualified quantities, rates and
   year-specific eligibility. Route each validated credit into Form 3800
   Part III with its form line, registration, passive classification, and
   credit-origin/election columns. Validate transfer-in amounts against
   Schedule A line 3, ownership, registration, signed binary, and Part III
   column (f). Never derive an EPE result from a blank election amount.
3. Finish income, deductions, Schedule SE, QBI, taxable income, Form 1040
   line 16, Schedule 2 line 1z, AMT, and earlier credits. Then compute Form
   3800 Part II limits and carryforward. Send **allowed** Form 3800 line 38
   to Schedule 3 line 6a and the 1040 credit resolver; reconcile tax before
   and after credit. This graph order prevents a self-declared Schedule 3
   credit from evading Form 3800 limits.
4. For Form 7205, derive the applicable **2026** §179D per-square-foot rate
   from the savings percentage and increased-rate evidence, then apply
   square footage, cost/prior-year limits, and the signed designer allocation.
   Form 7220 and certification evidence gates the increased-rate claim.
   Send the allowed deduction to one Schedule C activity, and recalculate
   profit, SE tax, half-SE deduction, AGI, QBI, and Form 3800 tax limit.
5. Render the complete PDF set and all required continuations/statements.
   Build MeF records and binary links from the same resolved graph, validate
   cross-form TINs/registration/amounts, then check current XSD and business
   rules. Compare every scenario 12 output with independently calculated
   fixture columns; record the packet's contradictions as source errors.

## Acceptance cases

- Earned 7207 credit, purchased 7207 credit, and transfer-out cases with
  distinct owners; a missing or unsigned Schedule A; mismatch of registration
  and amount; multiple credits/facilities requiring Form 3800 Part V.
- Ordinary and increased §179D rates at 25%, 50%, and 55% savings; designer
  allocation below the computed cap; prior-year deduction; construction
  before/after the June 30, 2026 cutoff; a missing Form 7220 apprenticeship
  part or qualified exception.
- Scenario 12 arithmetic with its **corrected** Form 3800 tax base $15,125
  and line 29 $5,125 on the packet's other assumptions, plus a separate
  source-conflict check for the printed $16,147/$6,147. Do not mark the return
  filed or the $8,095 refund accepted until credit provenance, QBI, signatures,
  and other gates in the fixture are resolved.
- TY2025 regression on its existing Form 3800/7207 behavior and TY2026
  full graph, PDF, MeF, XSD/rules, and ATS validation after the current MeF
  package is available.
