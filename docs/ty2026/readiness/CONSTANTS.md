# TY2026 constants and sources

Every TY2026 number lives in `forms/f1040/nodes/config/2026.ts`
(`config2026`), typed by `F1040Config` in `types.ts` and served to nodes as
`CONFIG_BY_YEAR[ctx.taxYear]`. `config2025` has the same fields, so a node
that reads config gets the right year without branching. `2026.test.ts`
checks each group against its source. Nodes still holding 2025 literals are
listed by section in `forms/f1040/2026/README.md`.

"RP" is [Rev. Proc. 2025-32](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf);
"OBBBA" is [P.L. 119-21](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ21/html/PLAW-119publ21.htm).

## Law changes that need year modules, not config

- Form 8962: no repayment limitation after 2025 (OBBBA §71305; RP §2.04) and
  no credit above 400% FPL (draft Form 8962 line 6).
- Schedule A: charitable floor ordering and carryforward (§170(b)(1)(I),
  §170(d)(1)(C)); the §68 reduction applies after every other limit.
- Form 1040 topology: lines 11a-11b, 12f, 13a-13b, 24a-24c, 30, 32a-32c,
  Schedule 3-A and Form 1062 ([form delta](./FORM-DELTA.md)).
- Mileage: TY2026 miles must be split at July 1 to apply the two rates.
- Trump account pilot: §6434 treats $1,000 as a payment for an eligible child
  born 2025-2028; it is paid to the account, not refunded on the return.

  config holds the statutory 2/37. Check the final Schedule A instructions.
