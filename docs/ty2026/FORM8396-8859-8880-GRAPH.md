# TY2026 mortgage, DC carryforward and saver-credit contracts

The pinned [2026 Form 8396](corpus/draft/f8396.pdf), [Form
8859](corpus/draft/f8859.pdf) and [Form 8880](corpus/draft/f8880.pdf)
each embed their 2026 instructions in the draft PDF. Their SHA-256 hashes
are in the [corpus manifest](corpus/manifest.json); the corresponding
[26](pdf-fields-f8396.csv), [9](pdf-fields-f8859.csv) and
[23](pdf-fields-f8880.csv) terminal widgets are inventoried. All three
produce nonrefundable credits, but have different eligibility, limit and
carryforward rules. Do not treat an upstream tentative amount as an allowed
Schedule 3 credit.

## Form 8396 — mortgage interest credit

| Lines | Source and 2026 route |
| --- | --- |
| Header, 1–3 | Require the state/local qualified Mortgage Credit Certificate (issuer, number, date and qualifying main-home address), Form 1098 or lender interest, certified indebtedness share, and certificate rate **10%–50%**. Exclude related-person interest and nonqualifying certificates. For a rate above 20%, cap line 3 at $2,000 (or co-owner share); a reissued certificate has an additional original-loan limit and may need a split-year statement. |
| Schedule A | Reduce deductible home-mortgage interest by **line 3**, even when the credit is limited and carried forward. This handoff must precede final Schedule A deduction/1040 tax, while credit limitation follows tax; plan the graph so it does not form a cycle. |
| 4–9 | Keep **2023, 2024 and 2025** carryforwards in separate origin-year buckets (lines 4–6). Compute the line 8 Credit Limit Worksheet after 1040 line 18 and specified earlier credits, including the Schedule 8812 Worksheet B substitution where directed. Line 9, limited by line 7 and line 8, goes to 2026 Schedule 3 line 6g. |
| 10–17 | Apply the printed current-year-then-oldest-prior-year use order and three-year expiration. Part II explicitly identifies 2025, 2024 and 2026 balances to carry to 2027; 2023 unused credit drops out. No amount above the high-rate $2,000 cap is ever a carryforward. |

The shared [Form 8396 node](../../forms/f1040/nodes/intermediate/forms/form8396/index.ts)
accepts one aggregate carryforward, skips the line 8 worksheet, sends its
unlimited tentative sum straight to Schedule 3 line 6g, and does not reduce
Schedule A interest or emit a printable 8396 record. Its MCC rate permits
0–100%, broader than the printed 10%–50% range. TY2025 PDF/MeF descriptors
are baselines only; there is no 2026 registration or filed output.

## Form 8859 — DC homebuyer credit carryforward only

The draft permits **only** the unused amount on 2025 Form 8859 line 4;
there is no new-home purchase credit in 2026. Line 2 comes from its tax
liability worksheet after the listed higher-priority credits. Line 3 is
min(line 1, line 2) and goes to 2026 Schedule 3 line 6h. Line 4 carries
the unused balance to 2027; its embedded instruction says it can continue
until used and cannot be carried back. Reconcile the source against the
filed 2025 return, not a free-form claimed opening balance.

The shared [f8859 source node](../../forms/f1040/nodes/inputs/f8859/index.ts)
emits a pending flag and aggregate carryforward to the TY2025 finalization
path. The 2026 Schedule 3 explicitly rejects the unresolved flag. Build a
2026 credit-limit resolver and printable lines 1–4 before registering this
node. Audit its TY2025 PDF/MeF descriptors against current 2026 widgets and
XML/rules.

## Form 8880 — 2026 saver’s credit

| Lines | Source and 2026 route |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Check taxpayer and spouse separately: birth before January 2, 2009, nondependent and not a full-time student for five calendar months. Use 1040 line 11a with the embedded foreign-income/housing/Puerto Rico/American Samoa adjustment; apply the printed filing-status table through $40,250 single/MFS/QSS, $60,375 HOH and $80,500 MFJ. |
| 1–6 | Add eligible IRA/Roth and designated-beneficiary ABLE contributions to elective and voluntary plan deferrals by person. Exclude rollovers and employer contributions. Reduce by applicable distributions received **after 2023 through the 2026 return due date including extensions**; on MFJ, include both spouses' distributions in both columns unless the printed prior-year joint-return exception applies. Cap each person's line 6 at $2,000. |
| 7–12 | Apply the 50%/20%/10% status-and-income rate table to combined eligible contributions. Compute the line 11 Credit Limit Worksheet after the specified earlier credits, including Schedule R, then send line 12 to 2026 Schedule 3 line 4. The current form still covers saver’s credit; its embedded note says the saver’s match begins with **2027** returns. |

The shared [Form 8880 node](../../forms/f1040/nodes/intermediate/forms/form8880/index.ts)
has correct-looking 2026 threshold config, but defaults missing AGI/status to
zero/single, takes unconstrained tax credit if the limit is omitted, lacks
age/dependency/student and ABLE source facts, and does not implement the
joint-distribution lookback exception. Its printable record covers only a
subset of form lines. Audit TY2025 PDF/MeF against the 23 current widgets;
the node is not registered in TY2026.

## Build order and acceptance

1. Verify final 2026 forms and current MeF XSD/rules; keep the pinned draft
   instruction pages as the line and worksheet baseline.
2. Build source-keyed MCC/1098 and contribution/distribution records plus
   2025 filed-return carryforward imports. Calculate 8396's Schedule A
   interest reduction before tax; calculate all three credit limits after
   preceding credits in their printed priority order.
3. Produce full form lines and 2027 carryforward ledgers; reconcile 8396
   line 9 to Schedule 3 line 6g, 8859 line 3 to 6h, and 8880 line 12 to
   Schedule 3 line 4. Reject unknown MCCs, missing origin years and absent
   saver eligibility rather than accepting an unbounded amount.
4. Render complete 2026 PDFs and current MeF documents/statements. Test
   final XSD and active rules against whole returns; preserve TY2025
   regressions for shared nodes.
5. Cover MCC rate 20%/above 20%, co-owner/refinance, Schedule A reduction
   with zero allowed credit, 2023 expiration, DC zero-liability and perpetual
   carryforward, saver age/student/dependent, ABLE contribution, both-spouse
   distribution lookback, each 2026 rate boundary and zero-tax-limit cases.

These are research contracts. None of the three complete 2026 filing routes
is registered or MeF-verified yet.
